At 16x16 grid: max |dE/E| = 5.43e-03 (FAIL)

## OPEN: Energy measurement: support mixed_* Hamiltonian operators

**Status:** Open (enhancement)
**Priority:** Medium
**Labels:** enhancement, energy-measurement

The energy measurement module (`tidal/measurement/energy.py`) cannot handle `mixed_1_0_0_1` (time-space cross derivative d_t d_z) and `mixed_2_0_0_0` (second time derivative d^2_t) operators in Hamiltonian terms.

Error: `Unknown spatial operator for energy measurement: 'mixed_1_0_0_1'`

**Affected examples:**
- `gravitational_waves/` (3+1D, unreduced)
- `gravitational_waves_1d/` (1+1D, plane-wave reduced)

This is NOT a regression from plane-wave reduction — it's a pre-existing limitation of the energy measurement code, which only supports `identity`, `gradient_*`, `laplacian_*`, and `time_derivative` operators.

**Impact:** Simulations run correctly. Energy conservation cannot be verified via `tidal measure` for GW examples.

**Fix:** Extend `_gradient_product_density` in `energy.py` to handle:
1. `mixed_N_0_0_0` -> Nth time derivative (resolve via velocity state)
2. `mixed_1_0_0_1` -> time-space cross derivative (d_t applied to spatial gradient)
