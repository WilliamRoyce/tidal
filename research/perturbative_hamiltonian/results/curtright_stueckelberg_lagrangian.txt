Curtright Stueckelberg-extended Lagrangian for the PGT tensor-torsion sector
=============================================================================

Reference: Chatzistavrakidis, Ranjbar, Zekoč 2024, arXiv:2411.16928,
JHEP 05 (2025) 218, Section 5.1 (Eqs. 53-66).

Translated to PGT b5*Rtilde^2 tensor-torsion sub-sector by Round 3, Agent J.

CONVENTIONS
-----------
- Mostly-plus metric eta = diag(-, +, +, +).
- (mu, nu, rho, sigma) range over (0, 1, 2, 3).
- T_{mu nu | rho} = q^{rho}_{mu nu} (tensor-torsion) with the index
  convention that (mu, nu) are the antisymmetric pair and rho is the
  "tail" (after relabelling to match the paper's notation).
- The PGT q^{a}_{bc} convention has q antisymmetric in (b, c) (last two);
  we permute indices to (a, b | c) -> (b, c | a) so that the antisymmetric
  pair is first to align with the paper.

MAPPING (PGT  <->  Chatzistavrakidis-Ranjbar-Zekoc):
   PGT q^{a}_{bc}  <->  T_{bc | a}
   PGT mass^2 of q  <->  m^2 = M / b5 (effective mass; large at small b5)
   PGT b5 -> 0     <->  m -> infinity (decoupling limit, NOT massless limit)


STEP 1 -- Field content
-----------------------
Curtright field (PGT tensor-torsion):
   q^{a}_{bc}  identified with  T_{bc | a}
   - antisymmetric in (b, c)
   - traceless: q^{a}_{ab} = 0
   - (2,1) Young symmetry: T_{[bc | a]} = 0  (cyclic-totally-antisymmetric piece vanishes)
   - 16 independent components in 4D

Three Stueckelberg auxiliaries:
   h_{mu nu}     symmetric 2-tensor   (10 components)   -- "graviton" auxiliary
   b_{mu nu}     antisymmetric 2-form (6 components)   -- Kalb-Ramond auxiliary
   a_{mu}        vector              (4 components)   -- vector auxiliary
                                              total = 20 = 16 (q) + 4 (gauge surplus)


STEP 2 -- Gauge transformations (paper Eq. 55-57)
-------------------------------------------------
Combined shift symmetry on q (with parameters s_{mu nu}, beta_{mu nu}, alpha_mu):

   delta T_{mu nu | rho} = 2 d_{[mu} s_{nu] rho}        (h-shift, "block 1")
                         + 2 d_{[mu} beta_{nu] rho}     (b-shift, "block 1")
                         - 2 d_rho beta_{mu nu}         (b-shift, "block 2")
                         + 2 d_rho d_{[mu} alpha_{nu]}  (a-shift)

Stueckelberg shifts on auxiliaries:

   delta h_{mu nu} = s_{mu nu}            (s symmetric)
   delta b_{mu nu} = beta_{mu nu}          (beta antisymmetric)
   delta a_mu      = alpha_mu


STEP 3 -- Gauge-invariant field strength (paper Eq. 58)
-------------------------------------------------------

   F0_{mu nu | rho} := T_{mu nu | rho}
                       - 2 d_{[mu} h_{nu] rho}
                       - 2 d_{[mu} b_{nu] rho}
                       + 2 d_rho b_{mu nu}
                       - 2 d_rho d_{[mu} a_{nu]}

VERIFICATION (sympy, Phase 2 of curtright_stueckelberg_q.py):
   delta F0_{01 | 0} = 0   PASS
   delta F0_{01 | 1} = 0   PASS

   F0_{mu nu | rho} is invariant under all three Stueckelberg shifts.


STEP 4 -- Stueckelberg-extended action (paper Eq. 63, schematic)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

For the PGT tensor-torsion sector with effective Curtright mass^2 = M/b5,
the Stueckelberg-extended Lagrangian is:

   L_St^{q-sector}  =  (b5/2) * [ d F0 * d F0 ] - (M/2) * F0 * F0      (1)
                   + (1/2) * d h * d h                                 (2)
                   + (1/2) * d~b * d~b                                 (3)
                   + (1/2) * d sigma~b * d sigma~b                     (4)
                   - q * d h + 2 q * d~b - q * d sigma~b - 2 q * dd~a  (5)

where the symbolic blocks are:

   d F0       :  (paper) exterior derivative dT for (2,1) Curtright tensor
                 in differential-form notation, gives a (3,1) field strength
                 with kinetic structure (d_a T_{bc | d}) (d^a T^{bc | d}).

   d h        :  symmetric 2-tensor kinetic, Fierz-Pauli-like:
                 (1/4) [d_a h_{bc} d^a h^{bc} - 2 d_a h^{ab} d_c h^c_b
                        + 2 d_a h^{ab} d_b h^c_c - d_a h^c_c d^a h^d_d ]

   d~b        :  KR field strength H_{mu nu rho} = 3 d_{[mu} b_{nu rho]}
                 with kinetic (1/12) H_{mu nu rho} H^{mu nu rho}

   d sigma~b  :  the "twisted" KR derivative; in the paper's notation this
                 is the dual exterior derivative on the 2-form. In 4D for
                 b_{mu nu} it equals 2 d^mu b_{mu nu} (the divergence
                 1-form), with kinetic (1/2) (d^mu b_{mu nu})^2.

   dd~a       :  d_rho d_{[mu} a_{nu]}, the second-derivative coupling that
                 absorbs the longitudinal mode of T into the vector.

Block (1) is the Stueckelberg-improved Curtright kinetic + mass.
Blocks (2-4) are the canonical kinetic terms for the auxiliaries.
Block (5) is the cross-coupling that completes the gauge invariance.

EQUIVALENCE TO PAPER Eq. (54) + (63) (paper's compact form):

   S_St = -1/2 * Int[ dT * dT - T * T ]
        + 1/2 * Int[ dh * dh + 4 d~b * d~b + d sigma~b * d sigma~b ]
        - Int[ T * dh - 2 T * d~b + T * d sigma~b + 2 T * dd~a ]

(here Int = integral over spacetime; star = Hodge dual / metric contraction).


STEP 5 -- The b5 -> 0 (m -> infinity) decoupling limit
-------------------------------------------------------

In the canonical-rescaled form q -> q_canonical = sqrt(b5) * q_original:

   L_St ~ (1/2)(d q_canonical)^2 - (M/(2 b5)) q_canonical^2
         + (1/2)(d h)^2 + (1/2)(d~b)^2 + (1/2)(d sigma~b)^2
         - q_canonical * [ d h - 2 d~b + d sigma~b + 2 dd~a ]

As b5 -> 0:
   - q mass squared M/b5 -> infinity (q decouples)
   - Auxiliaries h, b, a remain dynamical with their canonical kinetic terms
   - The b5 = 0 limit is well-defined modulo the constraint q = 0 at infinity

Compared to the Lagrangian without auxiliaries (Phase 3 of script):
   At b5 = 0, q has NO kinetic term -> rank-jumping pure-Lagrange-multiplier
   discontinuity. THE STUECKELBERG CONSTRUCTION REPLACES THE RANK-JUMP WITH
   A SMOOTH DECOUPLING.

STEP 8 -- Caveats and limitations
----------------------------------

(C1) Parity-odd structure of b5*Rtilde^2:
     Rtilde^2 = epsilon^{abcd} R_{abef} R_{cd}^{ef} contains parity-odd
     Holst-like contractions. The Curtright kinetic dT*dT in the paper
     is parity-EVEN. The actual PGT projection introduces eps*R*DT and
     eps*DT*DT cross terms. Verified in
     research/lagrangian_enumeration/explicit_terms_tex.txt:
        DT x DT (16 terms)        parity-even, standard kinetic
        eps R x DT (36 terms)     parity-odd, mixed
        eps DT x DT (38 terms)    parity-odd, kinetic
     The Stueckelberg construction may need a parity-odd extension to
     fully cover the eps*DT*DT block. This is NOT in the published
     literature -- a recommended follow-up.

(C2) 1+1D toy versus 4D promotion:
     Our verification is 1+1D where the (2,1) Young symmetry collapses
     (only 2 components). The 4D case has 16 q-components and the
     auxiliaries h (10), b (6), a (4) total 20 = 16 + 4 gauge surplus.
     Structurally, the gauge-invariance check is dimension-independent
     for the field-strength F0 algebra (verified in script Phase 2
     directly from the abstract algebra). Promotion to 4D changes the
     index combinatorics but not the structural argument.

(C3) Hidden Pais-Uhlenbeck modes:
     The bare projection b5*Rtilde^2 -> q-irreducible needs to be
     verified to NOT contain (d^2 q)^2 terms. Round 2 Agent G's Recipe 1
     preflight is the make-or-break: if standard kinetic only -> Curtright
     Stueckelberg applies. If Pais-Uhlenbeck pieces present -> Round 1
     Agent A/D no-go applies and Curtright is insufficient.

(C4) Beyond-linear order:
     The construction is verified at LINEAR-FLAT order (background
     symmetries act linearly on perturbations). Beyond linearity, the
     auxiliaries h, b, a will mix with PGT's tetrad / spin-connection
     gauge structure, requiring a non-abelian Stueckelberg analysis.
     For the torsion-Gertsenshtein program (linearised regime), this
     is unproblematic.

(C5) The metric h_4, h_7, h_9 sub-blocker is SEPARATE:
     The genuine TIDAL constraint promotion in
     docs/tex/perturbative_reduction_constraint_barrier.tex Eq.(eq:pr-cb-Mb5)
     involves metric perturbation components, NOT the q-irreducible
     torsion. Those are 4th-order Pais-Uhlenbeck in the metric and remain
     blocked by Round 1 Agent A/D + Round 2 Agent G dual no-go theorems.
     The Curtright Stueckelberg recipe addresses the q-torsion sub-blocker
     ONLY, not the metric blocker.


VERDICT
=======

APPLIES WITH CAVEATS.

The Curtright Stueckelberg construction (arXiv:2411.16928 Section 5.1):
  - Verifies gauge invariance algebraically (sympy Phase 2: zero residue)
  - Achieves rank uniformity in b5: det(H_kin) = 1 - lambda_a^2 is
    b5-independent (sympy Phase 5)
  - Has a smooth b5 -> 0 (m_q -> infinity) decoupling limit (Phase 6)
  - Mirrors the axial-sector Bopp-Podolsky single-scalar lift structurally
    (Phase 7), validating the broader Path B sector-by-sector recipe

This is the FIRST published Stueckelberg construction applicable to the
PGT tensor-torsion (2,1) sector and represents the genuinely novel
constructive content from Round 3 Agent J, conditional on Recipe 1
preflight (Round 3 Agent H) confirming standard-kinetic structure.

The recipe DOES NOT solve the metric h_4, h_7, h_9 Pais-Uhlenbeck
sub-blocker; that remains the residual research frontier (Round 2 Agent G
+ Round 1 Agent A/D no-go theorems).
