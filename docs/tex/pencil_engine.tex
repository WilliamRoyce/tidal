\section{The Matrix-Pencil Engine and Gauge-Oblivious Simulation}
\label{sec:pencil-engine}

This section is the design record for the modal solver's constraint
machinery as rebuilt in August 2026 (GH \#457 and its chain \#458--\#460,
\#465, \#467, \#468, \#470, \#473, \#474, \#477). It records the theory, the
engine, the classification rule it depends on, the alternatives that were
measured and rejected, and the architecture that lets TIDAL evolve theory
input whose gauge structure the user does not know in advance. It
supersedes the constraint-handling prose of \cref{sec:modal-solver}
(Schur levels, the $|\lambda| > 10^{12}$ gauge filter, the jerk
substitution), which is kept there as history.

\subsection{Why a pencil}
\label{sec:pencil-why}

After the Fourier transform every mode $k$ of a linear, time-independent
system is a first-order linear differential--algebraic equation (DAE)
\begin{equation}
  B(k)\,\dot y = A(k)\,y ,
  \label{eq:pencil-dae}
\end{equation}
over the state slots $y = (q, v, \ldots)$. Rows are equations: a
kinematic row $\dot q = v$ has $B_{qq} = 1$, $A_{qv} = 1$; a dynamical
row carries the row's \emph{kinetic content} on the $B$ side (the
diagonal kinetic coefficient, and every $\texttt{d2\_t}$ cross term as
an off-diagonal $B$ entry) and its stiffness/damping content on the $A$
side; an algebraic row has a zero $B$ row. Putting all mass content on
$B$ is not a convenience: it is what makes the \#444/\#458 defect class
--- forgetting a row's $M^{-1}$ when folding a constraint coupling into
an already-scaled velocity row --- impossible by construction, because
no row is ever pre-scaled.

The pair $(A, B)$ is a \emph{matrix pencil} $\lambda B - A$. Its
generalized eigenvalues split into \emph{finite} ones (the dynamics) and
\emph{infinite} ones (instantaneous algebraic relations). For the
homogeneous free evolution, the infinite sector is exactly $z_i \equiv 0$:
writing the ordered generalized Schur form
$Q^H A Z = S$, $Q^H B Z = T$ with the finite block leading, the trailing
block obeys $T_{ii}\dot z_i = S_{ii} z_i$ with $T_{ii}$ nilpotent and
$S_{ii}$ invertible, which forces $z_i = 0$. The finite deflating
subspace $\mathrm{span}(Z_f)$ therefore \emph{is} the constraint
manifold, with an orthonormal basis, and the reduced generator is a
triangular solve,
\begin{equation}
  G = T_{ff}^{-1} S_{ff}, \qquad
  A_\text{eff} = Z_f\, G\, Z_f^H, \qquad
  P = Z_f Z_f^H ,
  \label{eq:pencil-deflation}
\end{equation}
so that $\dot y = A_\text{eff}\, y$ on the manifold and $P$ projects
initial data onto it. Off-manifold components are annihilated by
$A_\text{eff}$ and stay frozen. Because $Q$ and $Z$ are unitary and the
only inversion is triangular, the conditioning argument that retired the
eigendecomposition evolution path in v0.31 (\cref{sec:modal-robust-matrix-exp};
$\mathrm{cond}(V)$ blow-up, Higham 2008 \S2.3) does not apply here.

The pencil is row-equilibrated before any factorization (each equation
scaled to unit maximum magnitude). This is a left preconditioner: it
changes neither the solution set nor the right deflating subspaces, and
without it rows carrying small physical scales (a $B_0^2$-weighted
constraint) make every rank and magnitude decision unreliable --- the
same scale blindness as the determinant-gated regularizer of \#459.

\subsection{The engine}
\label{sec:pencil-engine-impl}

\texttt{\_pencil\_deflate} (\texttt{tidal/solver/modal.py}) is the single
composition engine for every constrained or mass-singular system. Its
contract:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Regularity probe.} The smallest singular value of
    $\lambda_0 B - A$ at two fixed generic probe points decides whether
    the pencil is regular. Per-pair $(\alpha, \beta) \approx (0, 0)$
    tests were rejected (\cref{sec:pencil-rejected}).
  \item \textbf{Gauge quotient} (\cref{sec:pencil-quotient}) when the
    probe finds near-null directions at the current determination floor
    $\tau$.
  \item \textbf{Ordered QZ} with the finite/infinite predicate
    $|\beta| > \tau\,(|\alpha| + |\beta| + \tau)$.
  \item \textbf{Deflation contract.} The construction is accepted only
    if it satisfies its own defining equation,
    \begin{equation}
      \frac{\lVert B Z_f G - A Z_f \rVert}{\lVert A Z_f \rVert}
      \le \max(10^{-8}, \tau) .
      \label{eq:pencil-contract}
    \end{equation}
    A second, reordering-free rung (right eigenvectors of the retained
    spectrum, $G$ from least squares) is tried under the same contract
    before refusing.
  \item \textbf{Adaptive determination floor.} $\tau$ starts at
    $n \cdot 10^{-12}$ and is raised decade by decade to $10^{-4}$
    until the contract passes; a raised floor is reported in a warning
    naming the final $\tau$. Content determined more weakly than $\tau$
    is treated as gauge and pinned (\cref{sec:pencil-quotient}). This
    resolved the nonminimal T4 point (\#467) at a residual of
    $4\times10^{-11}$, where zeroing by a fixed classifier violated the
    equations at $O(1)$.
  \item \textbf{Honest refusal.} If no floor yields a
    contract-consistent operator, \texttt{SingularPencilError} carries
    the diagnosis (``no double-precision operator is both faithful to
    the equations and integrable''); the stability probe converts it to
    the verdict \emph{not simulatable as posed}. Refusal is preferred to
    the pre-\#457 behavior of returning a plausible but wrong trajectory
    (\#455: an operator whose spectral abscissa grew with resolution).
\end{enumerate}

A fast path keeps the common case cheap: when $B$ is invertible for
every mode the batched solve $B^{-1}A$ is mathematically identical to
the deflation and is used instead; singular $B$ routes through the
engine. The convolution (position-dependent) path assembles one pencil
over all modes and, for specs with an implicit-dynamical sector, treats
the residual algebraic constraints as pencil \emph{slots} with pure-$A$
rows (``constraints as slots''); this collapsed the E.cal pencil's
dynamic range from $5.4\times10^{6}$ to~1 and made the former deferred
acceleration substitution exact.

\subsection{The implicit-dynamical sector}
\label{sec:pencil-classification}

\texttt{time\_derivative\_order} states the true left-hand-side fact of a
row; it does not guarantee the row is algebraic. The one definition of
the split is \texttt{EquationSystem.implicit\_dynamical\_sector} (renamed from the
historical \texttt{second\_order\_sector} on 2026-08-28;
\texttt{StateLayout.implicit\_dynamical\_fields} likewise), with two
rules:

\begin{description}
  \item[Rule 1 (inter-constraint time chains).] An order-0 row that
    references a time derivative of another order-0 field (a
    $\texttt{d2\_t}(C)$ mass coupling or a $\texttt{first\_derivative\_t}(C)$ /
    $v_C$ damping coupling) is secretly differential. Both endpoints are
    promoted, and the closure is taken over connected components of the
    time-edge graph. Folding such rows as algebraic was the \#457 defect,
    measured at $O(1)$ residuals on 20 shipped specs.
  \item[Rule 2 (dynamical-row accelerations).] An order-0 field whose
    \emph{acceleration} any dynamical row references is promoted, because
    algebraic recovery cannot supply $\ddot q$ of an eliminated field
    (the twice-differentiated recovery is jerk-level through the velocity
    columns); promotion turns the term into ordinary $B$-side mass
    content. Velocity references from dynamical rows deliberately do
    \emph{not} promote --- $\dot C = R\,\dot y$ is exact as a $B$-side
    fold. Promotion happens precisely where elimination cannot be exact.
\end{description}

Promoted fields receive field and velocity slots, a zero kinetic
diagonal ($M_{ff} = 0$), and route through the engine; the time-domain
backends and \texttt{modal-jax} refuse them with an actionable message.
\emph{Vocabulary:} ``implicit-dynamical sector'' is this classification;
``$\varepsilon$-promoted'' is reserved for the perturbative promotion of
new degrees of freedom by $\tilde R^2$-class terms (\#321, \#464), a
different notion that must not share the word.

\subsection{Gauge quotient and its root-cause position}
\label{sec:pencil-quotient}

A singular pencil ($\det(\lambda B - A) \equiv 0$) has state directions
the equations never determine: the Kronecker column-chain space. At a
generic probe $\lambda_0$ the null vectors of $\lambda_0 B - A$ are
evaluations of the polynomial null family (for the byte-identical
equation triplets of \texttt{dark\_photon\_plasma}, GH \#465,
$x(\lambda) = q_\text{diff} + \lambda\, v_\text{diff}$), so the union of
null spaces over two probes spans the chain space $W$. The quotient
pins $W$ to zero --- the explicit, warned form of the minimum-norm
convention the pre-\#457 machinery applied silently through
\texttt{pinv} --- restricts the pencil to the complement, and reduces the
result to a square regular pencil handed to the deflation.

Two positions are recorded with it. First, spec-level redundancy is a
Wolfram-derivation artifact: the root fix is re-derivation of the theory
(\#374), and the quotient is the mathematically correct interim, per the
rule that broken symbolic output is never repaired in Python. Second,
the row compression assumes the restricted equations have exactly
$n_\text{red}$ independent combinations; that holds for exact chains but
\emph{fails for near-null quotients} of localized backgrounds, where it
discards 66--144 independent equations on the E.cal pencil and hands the
deflation a pencil that is singular at every $\lambda$ (\#474, measured
2026-08-27). That is the true mechanism of the \#468 refusal.

\subsection{Rejected alternatives}
\label{sec:pencil-rejected}

Each alternative below was implemented or derived and failed a measured
test; the record exists so none is re-proposed.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Velocity-aware Schur elimination ($R_x$, $R_v$).} The
    hand-derivation left a residual $\tilde D_{cc}\,\dot z_c$ that
    vanishes only when no fix is needed; a corpus scan found the rotated
    $\tilde D_{cc} = 0$ on every shipped spec, which made the formula
    corpus-correct but not general. Superseded by the engine, which has
    no per-case formulas.
  \item \textbf{Freeze-heuristic gauge completion.} Produced spurious
    eigenvalues of $2\times10^{8}$ on the \emph{decoupled} dark photon,
    which must be stable.
  \item \textbf{Per-pair $(\alpha,\beta)\approx(0,0)$ singularity test.}
    Misfires on regular pencils with $B_0^2$-scale rows (a pencil with
    smallest singular value $4\times10^{-5}$ was flagged singular).
  \item \textbf{$|\lambda|$ conditioning horizon.} Fires on harmless
    rounded-infinite pairs: the small-coupling nonminimal point was
    refused although its operator closed the oracle at $8\times10^{-11}$.
  \item \textbf{Data-derived gap classifier.} Cannot distinguish
    rounded-infinite from genuinely huge pairs (a mode-8/mode-9
    counterexample), and QZ reordering through hundreds of
    ill-conditioned infinite pairs wrecks the factorization (contract
    residual 2.66 measured).
  \item \textbf{Spatial gauge masking by field} (2026-08-27). Pinning the
    far-field values of the temporal-index metric components with a
    smooth least-squares normal form left the static residual gauge on
    the spatial components (8--24 directions below $10^{-8}$) and any
    smooth pin carries an inherent transition-zone continuum; refused at
    every $\tau$.
  \item \textbf{Single-probe paired reduction} (2026-08-27). Dropping the
    equations that determined the pinned directions yields a
    contract-consistent pencil (residual $3\times10^{-7}$) but the
    dropped rows are the far-field constraint chains, which carry
    velocity content; the retained system is a free evolution with
    constraint-violating modes ($\max\Re\lambda$ between 19 and
    $2\times10^{5}$, varying with $\tau$ and $N$).
  \item \textbf{Extended precision.} Deferred (\#470): it does not
    identify the meaningful sector and is needed only if the
    under-determined sector itself ever becomes the observable.
\end{enumerate}

The survivor is the deflation contract of \cref{eq:pencil-contract}: it
checks the object's own defining equation, so every failure mode above
manifests in it, whereas no spectral threshold could separate the
pathological cases from the harmless ones.

\subsection{Gauge-oblivious simulation: pin, certify, restrict}
\label{sec:pencil-general-input}

TIDAL must accept theory input whose gauge structure the user does not
know --- adding a term to a Lagrangian may break or keep a symmetry, and
Appendix~H of the thesis shows that even the Lorenz-class conditions of
linearized GR are theory-specific. Gauge fixing at derivation therefore
remains a user option (\cref{sec:gauge-fixing}) and never a requirement.
The architecture that meets this has one mechanism and three
identification routes, all feeding the one engine (never a solver fork):

\paragraph{Pin + certify.} Evolve the full mixed system; pin the
genuinely undetermined directions explicitly (the quotient's
minimum-norm zero, warned, and now \emph{recorded}: the engine's
\texttt{PencilDiagnostics} stores, per state slot and mode, the squared
row norm of the pinned subspace, written to the run's
\texttt{metadata.json} as \texttt{solver\_diagnostics.pin\_overlap}). At
measurement time \texttt{tidal measure} stamps every result with a
\texttt{gauge\_certificate}: a measurement whose field support carries no
pinned content is provably independent of the gauge choice
(\emph{certified}); otherwise it is \emph{flagged} with the overlap
magnitude and the slots involved. A flag is physics information ---
``this quantity is not an observable of your theory'' --- not a
failure. Pinning is without loss of generality for invariant
observables; the certificate verifies that per output instead of
trusting it globally, and guards the $\tau$ boundary where a direction
judged undetermined may be genuinely determined but tiny. The integrated
canonical energy of a gauge-invariant quadratic action on a periodic
domain is gauge-invariant (pure-gauge directions carry no energy; only
the local density is gauge-dependent), so on genuine pins the energy
certificate must pass --- it doubles as a check that the pinned
directions were pure gauge. The verdict is a \emph{direct} test: the
run stores real-space probes of its pinned subspace, and
\texttt{tidal measure} perturbs the state along them and re-runs the
measurement, reporting the relative sensitivity (``perturb and
remeasure''). This decides the cases a per-slot overlap cannot --- an
observable that reads a pinned field only through a combination its
operator annihilates, or a pinned combination that mixes a value with a
derivative. On the dark-photon CDT spec the $h_5 \to a_1$ conversion
and the total energy are certified (the slot overlap alone would have
flagged the energy), while a scalar reading the pinned torsion
component's raw value is flagged --- automating what the
FV$\leftrightarrow$CDT equivalence pin proved by hand
($4.6\times10^{-14}$).

\paragraph{Route 1 --- global spectral gap.} The uniform class: the
finite/infinite split has a gap and the quotient finds the pins.
Shipped; full mixing included.

\paragraph{Route 2 --- Kronecker-like staircase reduction (GH \#473):
BUILT, VERIFIED, AND CLOSED.} The idea was to extract the right singular
blocks $L_\varepsilon$ (gauge chains, $\varepsilon+1$ columns and
$\varepsilon$ rows), the left singular blocks $L_\eta^T$ (constraint
chains) and the regular part, with rank decisions at tolerance $\tau$ and
only orthogonal transformations (Van Dooren 1979; Demmel \&
K\aa gstr\"om's GUPTRI; \texttt{MatrixPencils.jl}'s \texttt{klf} as the
open reference implementation). A faithful port was written and
\emph{verified correct} --- kernel equivalence and structure
postconditions exact on random rank-deficient windows, a known scrambled
Kronecker canonical form recovered exactly, synthetic gates at machine
precision --- and it is preserved at
\texttt{scripts/research/klf\_staircase/}.

It does not deliver a usable operator on the localized class, and the
reason is not the implementation. Measured on E.cal at $N=16/24/32$ over
$\tau \in [10^{-12}, 10^{-5}]$: shallow $\tau$ marches the right-block
staircase through the physical sector (finite dimension collapses from 64
to $\le 14$), while deep $\tau$ leaves the weakly determined far-field
directions live with unphysical rates ($\max\Re\lambda \approx
+0.5\ldots+4.5$, fluctuating with $\tau$ and $N$) and $10^{-4}\ldots0.3$
cross-coupling into the physical block; the usable window moves with $N$.
The weak pairs carry $O(1)$ content on the $B$ side through their
velocity identities while the $\varepsilon$-weakness sits on the $A$ side,
so they can never present as right (no-$E$) chains at any float64
tolerance. This is the Kronecker form's known discontinuity made concrete:
the staircase computes the exact structure of a \emph{nearby} pencil, and
on a determination continuum ``nearby structure'' is $\tau$-unstable.

Two results survive. First, at deep $\tau$ the construction \emph{does}
certify a full-system operator (deflation contract $\sim\!10^{-10}$ where
ordered QZ measured $2.66$, with the $\{h_5, a_1\}$ block exact to
$\sim\!10^{-13}$): GH \#474's \emph{construction} defect is solved, and
identification was the deeper obstruction. Second, a durable semantic
point: the deflation contract certifies consistency with the
$\tau$-truncated equations, \emph{not} rate fidelity in the weakly
determined directions. A certified operator can still be honestly
unusable --- which is why the refusal-over-plausible-trajectory
discipline is right rather than over-cautious.

\paragraph{Route 0 --- symbolic gauge-structure discovery (GH \#477):
ATTEMPTED AND PARKED.} If tolerance cannot \emph{identify} the gauge
sector, the symbols can. Expanding the exact Noether identity
$E_i R^i{}_\alpha \equiv 0$ to first order about a background gives
\begin{equation}
  R^i{}_\alpha L_{ij} + E_i(\bar\Phi)\,\bigl(\partial_j R^i{}_\alpha\bigr) = 0 ,
  \label{eq:linearized-noether}
\end{equation}
with $E[\bar\Phi]$ the \emph{background EOM residual} and $L$ the
linearized operator. Since $L$ is the Hessian of a quadratic action, the
same coefficients give both the gauge orbit (right/column structure) and
the Noether identity (left/row structure), each annihilated up to a defect
\emph{exactly linear in the background-EOM violation}. Hence exact gauge
structure $\iff$ self-consistent background. E.cal violates that twice by
construction --- flat metric against EM stress $T^{\mathrm{EM}} \sim
B_0^2$, and a dual-Gaussian $B_0(z)$ requiring an external current
$J \sim \partial_z B_0$ --- which is the literature-standard
externally-imposed treatment, leaving the physics intact but making the
symmetry \emph{approximately} broken instead of an exact null space.

The identification half works, and was measured
(\texttt{scripts/research/gauge\_orbit/}): the hand-derived
diffeomorphism orbit chains annihilate the vacuum ($B_{\mathrm{peak}}=0$)
pencil at $10^{-17}$ for every gauge-profile mode at $N=16/24/32$; the
defect on the full pencil scales exactly as
\cref{eq:linearized-noether} demands (slope $1.000$ on the photon rows
from the Maxwell violation, $1.999$ on the metric rows from the Einstein
violation); and the symbolically derived orbit \emph{is} the numerically
weak sector --- the weakest right singular vectors of $A - \lambda B$ sit
within $6\times10^{-3}$ radians of the orbit span, and a grading told
nothing about $B_0(z)$ places the least-determined gauge profiles at zero
near-Gaussian energy share against $0.998$ for the most-determined. The
U(1) chain is the control and breaks at $O(1)$ even at
$B_{\mathrm{peak}}=0$, which correctly attributes that breaking to this
spec's Lorenz term in the action rather than to the background.

What fails is the \emph{pinning}, and this is a statement about the class
rather than about any construction. Two corrections came out of the
experiment, each caught by a control. Dropping rows to square up the
quotient is refuted: this pencil has no redundant rows (minimum row
singular value $0.62$) because it carries velocity-definition rows that no
Noether identity touches, so after pinning the column excess outruns the
row redundancy roughly five to one and any row removal discards real
equations. And the quotient must \emph{allow} the gauge drift and project
it out rather than forbid it --- forbidding it costs $0.21$ even when the
symmetry is exact. Corrected, the quotient is well-posed at $2\times
10^{-15}$ and discards no equation, so GH \#474's failure mode is
structurally impossible in it; the $\{h_5,a_1\}$ block is reproduced to
$10^{-15}$ with $10^{-14}$ cross-coupling. But the pinned directions carry
$\approx\!20\%$ of the true motion, $\tau$- and $N$-independently, against
$1.6\times10^{-15}$ in the exact-symmetry control. \emph{Weakly determined
is not weakly excited}: the far-field modes are nearly free, hence easy to
excite and large in amplitude, so pinning them to zero does not approximate
the dynamics --- it deletes a fifth of it.

Two consequences carry forward. No pinning-based construction can satisfy
a full-system contract on this class. And what such a construction
\emph{does} do exactly is evolve the decoupled physical sector --- which
reclassifies route 3 below: for the localized class the closure route is
not a rescue for a refusal but the correct treatment, because the
remaining directions carry genuine dynamics that pinning would fabricate.
No general float64 route is known; the surviving candidate is extended
precision (GH \#470), now with a measured target --- the determination
continuum is about eleven decades deep and $N$-independent.

\paragraph{Route 3 --- observable-sector closure (shipped, GH \#468).}
When the full pencil would refuse and the excited sector is exactly
closed, evolve that sector alone. For the localized class this is not a
fallback but the correct treatment, per the route-0 measurements above. \texttt{EquationSystem.dependency\_closure}
is the structural closure of ``the equation of $X$ references $Y$'';
\texttt{restrict\_spec\_dict} writes the closure as a first-class spec
JSON (\texttt{restricted\_spec.json}, the run's \texttt{spec\_path}),
re-validated by the loader, with fields outside the closure
\emph{absent} --- never present but wrong --- and provenance under
\texttt{metadata.restriction}. \texttt{tidal measure} refuses total energy
when Hamiltonian terms were dropped and conversion on omitted fields,
naming the sector alternative. Activation is a recovery from refusal
only; \texttt{--no-closure-restriction} attempts the full system. On the
frozen Phase~E geometry the closure-evolved E.cal conversion reproduces
the path-integrated Boccaletti prediction to $0.25\%$
($P_\text{peak} = 0.003912$ vs.\ $\sin^2 0.0627 = 0.003922$),
independent of $t_\text{end}$; the archived $0.0036$ was the
defective-operator value (\texttt{docs/RESULTS\_AMENDMENTS.md},
Amendment~2).

\paragraph{Why the far field is hostile.} For a localized background the
gauge-class rows read $B_0(x)^2\, g(x) + [\,O(1)\text{ operator on other
fields}\,] = 0$: the symmetry the background breaks returns wherever the
background vanishes. On the Phase~E geometry $B_0(x)^2$ spans ten decades
across the grid and is below $10^{-11}$ of the $O(1)$ row terms on
$\sim\!40\%$ of the points, independent of $N$. The weak directions are
the full linearized-diffeomorphism orbit --- $(h_0, h_3, v_{h_3})$,
$(h_1, h_6, v_{h_6})$, $(h_2, h_8, v_{h_8})$, all far-field localized ---
which is why every setup with a background that vanishes somewhere
(localized magnetic fields, plasma profiles) is in this class, and why
the closure route exists rather than a per-theory gauge choice. The
2026-08-26 symbolic experiment (GH \#477) confirmed this orbit
analytically and added the reason it cannot simply be pinned away: these
directions carry a fifth of the true motion.

\subsection{Historical audit and replaced machinery}
\label{sec:pencil-audit}

The rework replaced: the SVD mass-Schur branch with the \#260 $V_\text{eff}$
back-projection (uniform deflation; pinned by the critical-point
continuity tests and the hand-solved rank-deficient-mass case); the
$K_{cc}$ minimum-norm \texttt{pinv} (the explicit quotient); the
determinant-gated $10^{-14}$ Tikhonov on $S_{cc}$ (rank-revealing
\texttt{pinv}, \#459 --- its $1/\epsilon$ round-off amplification had
produced a spurious $2\times10^{5}$ abscissa); the $k$-dependent
singular-mass bare \texttt{pinv} (dead code, \#460); the path-4 one-step
Newton for constraint accelerations (Rule~2 promotion); and the
post-hoc $A_{dc}$ corrections with the $B_\text{lhs}$ return contract of
\#256, whose only consumer was retired by the Pad\'e path (\#320). One
genuine narrowing is recorded: jerk-level content on singular-mass or
promoted systems now refuses instead of executing the old (wrong)
substitution (\#469). Nothing tested or reachable was lost; the two
pre-existing coverage holes found by the audit --- no in-suite
FV$\leftrightarrow$CDT pin and no \#263-class pin --- were closed or
filed.
