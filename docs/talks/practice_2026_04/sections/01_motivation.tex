\begin{frame}{The Gertsenshtein channel for HFGW detection}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item MHz band: no coherent detector.  Hypothesised sources --- early-universe processes, primordial-black-hole mergers and evaporation.

    \item \textbf{Gertsenshtein effect} --- graviton $\leftrightarrow$ photon in a $B$-field --- proposed as a search channel: photon-background reanalyses [1,\,2]; dark-matter-cavity reinterpretations [3,\,4].

    \item Bare GR mixing length $\sim 10^{6}$\,km even at magnetar fields --- too long for any source.

    \item Beyond-GR gravity may amplify the conversion --- not previously computed.
  \end{itemize}

  \vfill

\end{frame}
