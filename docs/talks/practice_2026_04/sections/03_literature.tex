\begin{frame}{Literature \& why it's topical now}
  \note{\textasciitilde 4 min mark. Convince the audience this isn't a curiosity.}
  \textbf{A short timeline:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Gertsenshtein (1962)} --- original effect, conversion formula.
    \item \textbf{Boccaletti, De Sabbata, Fortini, Gualdi (1970)} --- rederivation, plasma corrections.
    \item \textbf{Raffelt \& Stodolsky (1988)} --- unified with axion--photon mixing; how most modern papers cite it.
    \item \textbf{2020s revival} --- high-frequency GW detection: Aggarwal \emph{et al.} \emph{Living Reviews} 2021; Berlin, Domcke, Schwetz \emph{et al.} (cavity haloscopes); Domcke \& Garcia-Cely 2021 (astrophysical conversion).
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{0.3em}
  \textbf{Why it matters in 2026:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Astrophysical $B$-fields (magnetars $\sim\!10^{15}$\,G, IGM, early universe) are a natural lab.
    \item The \emph{only} linear-in-$h$ EM coupling --- any HFGW cavity-detector uses this mechanism.
    \item Model-independent at the GR level $\Rightarrow$ clean place to look for \emph{model-dependent} BSM signatures.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
