\begin{abstract}
The Gertsenshtein effect --- the conversion of gravitational waves
into photons in a background magnetic field --- provides one of the
few channels through which high-frequency gravitational radiation
could be observed. Manual derivation of the conversion channel
across the operator space of Poincar\'e gauge theory (PGT) is
infeasible; we present \TIDAL{} (Tensor Integration and Derivation
for Any Lagrangian), a symbolic--numerical pipeline that derives
the linearised equations of motion of any quadratic gauge-gravity
Lagrangian and propagates them in a chosen background. We find
that torsion decouples from the Gertsenshtein channel at minimal
coupling, so the conversion structure is carried entirely by
non-minimal Lagrangian operators. The quadratic PGT action is
then swept through single-coupling, multi-coupling, and full
sector-closure parameterisations, with each configuration's
modification of the conversion channel quantified relative to the
Einstein--Maxwell baseline. These configurations admit both
ghost-free amplification and suppression of the Gertsenshtein
channel, with the $\chi_1$ derivative coupling identified as the
dominant amplifying operator.
\end{abstract}
