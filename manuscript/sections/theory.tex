\section{Poincar\'{e} gauge theory and the Gertsenshtein kernel}%
\label{Theory}

\paragraph*{Conventions}
We work in the metric signature $(-,+,+,+)$ with natural units
$\hbar = c = 1$. Greek indices $\mu,\nu,\dots$ denote spacetime
coordinate components and Latin indices $i,j,\dots$ denote Lorentz-frame
components~\cite{barker2024poincare}. The reduced Planck mass is $\MPl$ with
$\MPl^2 = (8\pi G)^{-1}$. Throughout we use the gravitational coupling
$\kappa$ defined by $\kappa^2 = 16\pi G$.\footnote{%
    Two distinct objects appear under the symbol $\kappa$ in the
    recent Gertsenshtein literature: the linearised
    graviton--photon vertex coupling adopted here, with
    $\kappa^2 = 16\pi G$ and $[\kappa] = [\mathrm{Mass}]^{-1}$, and
    the Einstein constant which we denote
    $\kappa_{(\mathrm{E})}\equiv 8\pi G$,
    $[\kappa_{(\mathrm{E})}] = [\mathrm{Mass}]^{-2}$, used as the
    prefactor of $\MAGFt{}$
    in~\cite{barker2024poincare,barker2023confinement,barker2024psalter}.
    The two normalisations are related by
    $\kappa^2 = 2\kappa_{(\mathrm{E})}$.
} This is the linearised graviton--photon vertex coupling, and the
field redefinition $\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu} = h_{\mu\nu}/\kappa$ used
in~\cite{lella2024constraining} converts the prefactor
$\kappa^{-2}\,\MAGFt{}$ into the canonical kinetic form
$\tfrac{1}{2}(\partial\tilde{h})^2$. The totally antisymmetric
Levi--Civita tensor $\varepsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$ is normalised to
$\varepsilon^{0123} = +1$.

\subsection{Field content and quadratic action}%
\label{PGTframework}
% Pedagogical sequence per genre_conventions_theory.md §(d) Steps (a)–(e).
% Target ~350 w.

% Step (a): Riemannian baseline (notation, not pedagogy).
\paragraph*{Riemannian baseline notation}
We follow~\cite{barker2024poincare} in distinguishing the Riemannian
Levi--Civita connection
\begin{align}
  \rCon{^\sigma_{\mu\nu}}
    = \tfrac{1}{2}\g{^{\sigma\rho}}\bigl(
        \PD{_\mu}\g{_{\nu\rho}} + \PD{_\nu}\g{_{\mu\rho}}
        - \PD{_\rho}\g{_{\mu\nu}}\bigr),
  \label{LeviCivita}
\end{align}
decorated with a ring, from the independent Riemann--Cartan
connection $\MAGA{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$ introduced below; the
corresponding covariant derivatives are denoted $\rD{_\mu}$ and
$\rcD{_\mu}$ (\cref{MixedCovariantDerivative}).

% Step (b): Promotion of connection to independent field. Palatini
% formulation declared explicitly.
\paragraph*{Independent connection in the Palatini formulation}
The Palatini
formulation~\cite{palatini1919deduzione,ferraris1982variational,barker2024palatini}
treats the metric and the affine connection
$\MAGA{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$ as independent variational fields.
PGT~\cite{utiyama1956invariant,kibble1961lorentz,sciama1962physical,hayashi1979new,trautman1972einstein,hehl1976spin,hehl1995metric,blagojevic2002gravitation}
is formulated in this way, with torsion and curvature appearing as
the field strengths of spacetime translations and rotations
respectively
(\cite{blagojevic2002gravitation,blagojevic2003lectures,blagojevicHehl2013,hehlobukhov2007cartan}
give textbook and lecture-note treatments;
\cite{barker2025psalter2,barker2025hamilcar,barker2024r2,barker2025metricaffine,barker2025irfound1,barker2025irfound2,legner2025torc,bahamonde2025torsion,karananas2024weyl}
cover recent applications, and~\cite{lin2019pgt,blagojevic2018hamiltonian}
develop the ghost-free and no-tachyon classifications of the
linearised propagator%

% Lin / Blagojevic ghost-free classifications before final

% verification has been done for the early-stage models; the
% later-stage models are pending.
). The full field-theoretic formalism and gauging conventions are collected in
\cref{PGTBackground}.

% Step (c): Paired curvature and torsion field-strength definitions.
\paragraph*{Field strengths: curvature and torsion}
The translational and rotational gauge field strengths are defined
by%~\cite{kibble1961lorentz,hehl1976spin,hehl1995metric,blagojevic2003lectures,hehlobukhov2007cartan,barker2024poincare}
\begin{align}
  \MAGFt{^\sigma_\mu_\nu_\rho}
    &\equiv 2\,\PD{_{[\mu}}\MAGA{^\sigma_{\nu]}_\rho}
       + 2\,\MAGA{^\sigma_{[\mu|\lambda|}}\MAGA{^\lambda_{\nu]}_\rho},
  \label{Curvature} \\
  \MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}
    &\equiv 2\,\MAGA{^\sigma_{[\mu\nu]}}.
  \label{Torsion}
\end{align}
Curvature reduces to its Riemannian limit when the antisymmetric
part of the connection vanishes, equivalently when the torsion
\cref{Torsion} vanishes.

% Step (d): General quadratic action with named couplings.
\paragraph*{General action}
The most general action of Poincar\'{e} gauge theory coupled to
electromagnetism, constructed from the gauge field
strengths $\MAGFt{^\sigma_{\mu\nu\rho}}$, $\MAGT{^\sigma_{\mu\nu}}$,
and $\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}$, decomposes into sector blocks catalogued
in~\cref{PGTEnumeration},
as~\cite{lin2019pgt,blagojevic2018hamiltonian,barker2024poincare,sezgin1980ghostfree,aoki2020nonlinear,bahamonde2025cubic}
\begin{multline}
  \act = \int \diff^4 x\,\sqrt{-g}\,\Bigl[
        \lag_\text{lin}
        + \lag_{\MAGFt{^2}}
        + \lag_{\MAGT{^2}}
        + \lag_{F^2}
        + \lag_{\MAGFt{}\FieldF{}} \\[-0.5em]
        + \lag_\text{odd}
        + \lag_\partial
        + \lag_\text{cubic}
        + \lag_\text{CS}
      \Bigr],
  \label{QuadraticAction}
\end{multline}
where $\lag_\text{lin}$ collects the linear
invariants~\cite{kibble1961lorentz,holst1995barbero,barbero1995real,immirzi1997real};
$\lag_{\MAGFt{^2}}$ the curvature-squared sector;
$\lag_{\MAGT{^2}}$ the torsion-squared sector;
$\lag_{F^2}$ the Maxwell kinetic term; $\lag_{\MAGFt{}\FieldF{}}$ the
curvature--electromagnetic cross-couplings; $\lag_\text{odd}$
the parity-odd quadratic operators~\cite{karananas2014parity,blagojevic2018hamiltonian};
$\lag_\partial$ the higher-derivative
extensions~\cite{aoki2020nonlinear}; $\lag_\text{cubic}$ the
cubic-order operators~\cite{bahamonde2025cubic}; and
$\lag_\text{CS}$ the Chern--Simons torsion--EM couplings
involving the gauge potential~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic,itin2004maxwell,rubilar2003torsion},
catalogued in~\cite{obukhov2024electrodynamics}.
The subsets surveyed in this work are reported in
\cref{SectorSurvey}.

\subsection{Linearisation around the magnetic background}%
\label{Linearisation}
% Target ~350 w. Per genre_conventions_theory.md §(f) linearisation
% sub-movement.

\paragraph*{Background and perturbation ansatz}
We linearise about a flat metric carrying a static magnetic field
along $\hat{x}$, with vanishing background torsion. In most of this
work the background is taken to be uniform,
$\bm{B} = \Bzero\,\hat{x}$, the standard Gertsenshtein geometry. We
also consider localised backgrounds with $\bm{B}$ confined to a
finite region along the propagation direction.
% ; the specific profile
% is introduced in \cref{Gertsenshtein} and used in \cref{Validation},
% with the conversion kernel generalising as discussed there.
The metric, four-potential, and torsion are decomposed as
\begin{gather}
  g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu},
  \quad
  A_\mu = \bar{A}_\mu + \delta A_\mu,
  \quad
  \MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu} = \delta\MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu},
  \label{PerturbationAnsatz} \\
  \bar{A}_\mu\,\diff x^\mu = -\Bzero\,z\,\diff y, \label{BackgroundPotential}
\end{gather}
with the torsion tensor $\MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$ treated as
an inherently perturbative field.

\paragraph*{Order-counting rule}
We work to linear order in $h_{\mu\nu}$, $\delta A_\mu$, and the
torsion fluctuation $\delta\MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$, and to first
order in $\Bzero$.

\paragraph*{Palatini connection decomposition}
The Palatini connection
decomposes\wb{also tell your Claude to prevent eq overflows, you could break this eq.}
as~\cite{hehl1976spin,shapiro2002torsion,hehlobukhov2007cartan,blagojevic2003lectures}
\begin{subequations}
\label{PalatiniDecomposition}
\begin{align}
  \MAGA{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}
    &= \rCon{^\sigma_\mu_\nu} + K^\sigma{}_{\mu\nu}, \\
  K^\sigma{}_{\mu\nu}
    &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl(
        \MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}
        + \MAGT{_\mu^\sigma_\nu}
        + \MAGT{_\nu^\sigma_\mu}
      \bigr),
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
the second term being the contortion tensor. The linearised connection
perturbation $\delta\MAGA{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$ therefore inherits
contributions from both the metric perturbation $h_{\mu\nu}$,
% through the Levi--Civita connection variation
% $\delta\rCon{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$,
and the torsion perturbation
$\delta\MAGT{^\sigma_\mu_\nu}$.
% , through the contortion variation
% $\delta K^\sigma{}_{\mu\nu}$.
The perturbation $h_{\mu\nu}$ here
carries the dimensionful $\kappa$ implicitly, and is related by the
field redefinition $\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu} = h_{\mu\nu}/\kappa$ to the
canonically-normalised perturbation
$\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}$~\cite{lella2024constraining} via
$g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + \kappa\,\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}$.
Self-consistency bounds on the linearisation are detailed in
\cref{PerturbativeRegime}.

\paragraph*{Linearised kinetic matrix}
After expanding $\sqrt{-g}\,\lag$ to quadratic order in the
perturbation fields and reducing to plane-wave dependence
$\partial_x = \partial_y = 0$ along the propagation direction
$\hat{z}$, perpendicular to the background magnetic field
$\bm{B}$~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave,boccaletti1970conversion,raffelt1988mixing,cillis1996graviton,ejlli2020graviton,palessandro2023simple,palessandro2024gertsenshtein,lella2024constraining,domcke2025scattering}
% TODO: investigate WLOG vs not-WLOG status of this propagation
% direction for derivative / parity-odd PGT sectors (relates to
% issue #300 and the new "investigation: propagation-direction
% WLOG status" issue created with this revision).
with the photon perturbation in Lorenz gauge and the metric
perturbation evolved without a gauge constraint,\footnote{
Transverse-traceless-compatible initial conditions are imposed
in the vacuum region; the
evolution through the magnetic-field region is unconstrained,
allowing non-TT components to develop; see
\cref{TTGaugeAdmissibility}.} the surviving degrees of freedom
assemble into a quadratic kinetic system
\begin{align}
  \lag^{(2)} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\xi^\mathsf{T}\,
    \mathcal{K}(\partial_t,\partial_z)\,\xi,
  \label{KineticMatrix}
\end{align}
for a vector $\xi$ of metric, photon, and (where
propagating) torsion perturbations. The detailed structure of
$\mathcal{K}$ --- and the coupling between the metric, photon, and
torsion sectors --- depends on the Lagrangian chosen. Per-theory
results are presented in~\cref{Results}; explicit kinetic-matrix
outputs for representative theories are reported in
\cref{SymbolicMechanism}.

\subsection{Gertsenshtein conversion kernel}%
\label{Gertsenshtein}

\paragraph*{Electromagnetic-field conventions}
The electromagnetic sector is expressed in terms of the
four-potential $\FieldA{_\mu}$ and the gauge field strength
$\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}$, with background fields entering through the
background four-potential $\bar{A}_\mu$. The metric-dependent
contractions in the $\sqrt{-g}$ expansion incorporate the effects
of curved-spacetime electromagnetic-field
identification~\cite{hwangnoh2023emdef,hwangnoh2023graviton}. The
$\Bzero \to 0$ survey-coefficient framework used to extract
amplification ratios is set out in~\cref{SurveyCoefficient}.

\paragraph*{Conversion probability}
The conversion probability, defined as the energy fraction
transferred from the graviton sector to the photon sector over
the propagation distance $D$~\cite{raffelt1988mixing}, for
a graviton traversing a region of uniform $\Bzero$ takes
the closed form~\cite{boccaletti1970conversion,lella2024constraining}
\begin{align}
  \Pconv = \sin^2\!\left(\frac{\kappa\,\Bzero\,D}{2}\right),
  \label{GertsenshteinFormula}
\end{align}
which in the small-argument limit $\kappa\Bzero D \ll 1$ scales as
$\Pconv \propto \Bzero^2$, the characteristic Gertsenshtein
signature.\footnote{%
    The derivations of~\cite{palessandro2023simple,palessandro2024gertsenshtein}
    produce $\Pconv = \sin^2(\sqrt{G}\,\Bzero\,D)$, whose argument differs
    from~\cref{GertsenshteinFormula} by a factor of $\sqrt{4\pi}$;
    a further independent critique is provided by~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton}.
    An independent derivation in our convention is
    provided by~\cite{lella2024constraining}.
} For a finite magnetised region carrying a profile
$B_x(z)$ along the propagation direction,~\cref{GertsenshteinFormula}
generalises to the path-integrated Boccaletti
form~\cite{boccaletti1970conversion,raffelt1988mixing}
\begin{align}
  \Pconv = \sin^2\!\left(
    \frac{\kappa}{2}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\!B_x(z)\,\diff z\right),
  \label{BoccalettiPathIntegrated}
\end{align}
recovering~\cref{GertsenshteinFormula} when $B_x(z) = \Bzero$ is
constant over a length $D$. The regime of validity of these
linearised expressions in the torsionful background is set out
in~\cref{PerturbativeRegime}. \Cref{Validation} reproduces
both~\cref{GertsenshteinFormula} and~\cref{BoccalettiPathIntegrated}.
