\section{Symbolic outputs for the surveyed theories}%
\label{SymbolicOutputs}

\paragraph*{Purpose and reading guide}
The equations collected here are produced by the symbolic stage of
\cref{SymbolicComputation} for the theories under survey in
\cref{Results}, in the same flat-background, plane-wave-along-$z$ basis
used by every numerical run of \cref{NumericalEvolution}.
Each subsection corresponds to one theory: a single lead sentence
fixes the Lagrangian content, a figure float reproduces the full
equations of motion and Hamiltonian density emitted by the pipeline,
and (where applicable) a short labelled excerpt singles out an entry
that the main body cites by reference.
The intent is reference rather than narrative.
The Einstein--Maxwell baseline of \cref{SymbolicBaseline} fixes the
comparison standard against which the torsion subsections are read,
and the no-ghost diagnostic of \cref{SymbolicNonminimal} is the only
admissible interpretive aside in the appendix.

% --- E.1  Einstein--Maxwell Gertsenshtein baseline ------------------
\subsection{Einstein--Maxwell Gertsenshtein baseline}%
\label{SymbolicBaseline}
The pure Einstein--Maxwell theory $\mathcal{L} = \kappa^{-2}\mathcal{R}
- \tfrac{1}{4}F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$ on a uniform magnetic background
$\bm{B} = \Bzero\,\hat{x}$, linearised around flat spacetime with
the plane-wave ansatz of \cref{Linearisation} (propagation along
$z$, $\bm{B}$ transverse), gives the equations of
\cref{BaselineKineticOutput}.
The two transverse graviton polarisations are sourced linearly by
$\Bzero\,\partial_{z}$ derivatives of the transverse photon modes,
and the kinetic sub-matrix is block-diagonal in the two transverse
polarisation channels; the longitudinal photon modes decouple from
the graviton sector.
This is the comparison baseline for every torsion theory below.

\begin{figure*}[t]
\[
\begin{aligned}
\input{sections/appendices/listings/eom_baseline_full.tex}
\end{aligned}
\]
\caption{Lagrangian, equations of motion, and Hamiltonian density for
the Einstein--Maxwell theory on a uniform magnetic background,
linearised around flat spacetime with the plane-wave ansatz of
\cref{Linearisation}. The transverse graviton polarisations
$\mathcal{H}_{xy}$ and $\mathcal{H}_{yy}$ couple linearly to the
transverse photon modes $\mathcal{A}_{x}$ and $\mathcal{A}_{y}$
through the $\Bzero$ vertex; the longitudinal photon modes
$\mathcal{A}_{t}$ and $\mathcal{A}_{z}$ are free. The transverse
$h$ and $a$ modes exhaust the dynamical content of this theory.}%
\label{BaselineKineticOutput}
\end{figure*}

% --- E.2  Minimally-coupled Einstein--Cartan ------------------------
\subsection{Minimally-coupled Einstein--Cartan}%
\label{SymbolicReference}
The minimally-coupled Einstein--Cartan theory of
\cref{SectorSurvey} extends the baseline by three quadratic torsion
invariants with couplings $\alpha_{1}$, $\alpha_{2}$, $\alpha_{3}$
and no curvature-squared or nonminimal terms.
The equations of \cref{ReferenceKineticOutput} are the simplest
torsion-bearing kinetic system in this appendix; the additional
$\mathcal{T}$ modes enter as algebraic auxiliary fields satisfying
the constraint equations whose right-hand sides vanish.

\cref{BaselineKineticOutput} except for the linear constraint

\paragraph*{Observed coincidence in the $h_{\times}$ and $a_{x}$ kinetic
coefficients.}
The kinetic coefficients of the $h_{\times}$ and $a_{x}$ entries of
\cref{PropagatingKineticOutput} have been observed to coincide in
the symbolic output for every propagating-PGT theory the pipeline
has been run on; this coincidence is displayed in
\cref{HxAxIdentity}.
\begin{align}
K_{h_{\times} h_{\times}} \;=\; K_{a_{x} a_{x}} .%
\label{HxAxIdentity}
\end{align}

% --- E.4  Ricci--EM nonminimal coupling -----------------------------
\subsection{Ricci--EM nonminimal coupling}%
\label{SymbolicNonminimal}
The Ricci--EM nonminimal theory of \cref{SectorSurvey} adds a single
new vertex, $\delta_{1}\tilde{\mathcal{R}}_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$, on
top of the Einstein--Cartan terms. The resulting equations are
those of \cref{NonminimalKineticOutput}; the new vertex enters every
$h$-channel equation as an off-diagonal coupling proportional to
$\delta_{1}$ and reorganises the irreducible-torsion sector through
the second-time-derivative terms it introduces.

\paragraph*{Trace-channel kinetic excerpt.}
The diagonal entries of the kinetic matrix in the irreducible-torsion
basis $(\T[1]{}, \T[2]{}, \T[3]{})$ extracted from
\cref{NonminimalKineticOutput} are displayed in
\cref{TraceKineticOutput}; the sign of each entry is what the
no-ghost analysis below reads off.
\begin{align}
K_{\T[1]{}\T[1]{}},\quad K_{\T[2]{}\T[2]{}},\quad K_{\T[3]{}\T[3]{}} .%
\label{TraceKineticOutput}
\end{align}

\paragraph*{Sign-positivity of the irreducible torsion kinetic
matrices.}
The no-ghost condition on the irreducible-torsion sector is the
requirement that every diagonal entry of the kinetic matrix in the
$(\T[1]{},\T[2]{},\T[3]{})$ basis be positive in the metric signature of
\cref{Linearisation}; the four-parameter classification of
\citet{sezgin1980ghostfree} and its modern PGT refinements
\cite{lin2019pgt,barker2021hamiltonian} express the same condition as
algebraic inequalities on the Lagrangian couplings.
Read against \cref{TraceKineticOutput}, the surveyed Ricci--EM
nonminimal theory satisfies the no-ghost condition in the
$\T[1]{}$ and $\T[2]{}$ sectors but violates it in the trace
$\T[3]{}$ sector for $\delta_{1}\neq 0$; the kinematic observation
that this wrong-sign sector does not couple to the
$h_{\times} \leftrightarrow a_{x}$ Gertsenshtein channel is
recorded in \cref{ChannelStructure}.

campaign of \cref{Results}; \cref{GeneralNonminimalKineticOutput}

\paragraph*{Named parameter restrictions.}
The Bahamonde, Barker, and Shapiro variants surveyed in
\cref{SectorSurvey} are parameter-restricted instances of the
Lagrangian whose equations appear in
\cref{GeneralNonminimalKineticOutput}; their kinetic structure is
read off the same block under the corresponding substitutions in
the campaign manifest. Their dedicated subsections, when filled,
will state the restrictions in the form
$\beta_{1}=0,\ \xi=0,\ \ldots$ and refer back to
\cref{GeneralNonminimalKineticOutput} for the equations themselves.
