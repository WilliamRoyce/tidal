\section{Symbolic outputs for the surveyed theories}%
\label{SymbolicOutputs}

The surveyed theories are presented under a common reduction: the
linearised equations of motion are evaluated on a uniform magnetic
background $\bm{B} = \Bzero\,\hat{x}$ and dimensionally reduced to
plane waves propagating along $\hat{z}$ (\cref{Linearisation}).

% --- E.1  Einstein--Maxwell Gertsenshtein baseline ------------------
\subsection{Einstein--Maxwell Gertsenshtein baseline}%
\label{SymbolicBaseline}
The pure Einstein--Maxwell Lagrangian gives a block-diagonal kinetic
sub-matrix in the transverse photon channels of
\cref{BaselineKineticOutput}; discussed in \cref{Validation}.

% --- E.3  Minimally-coupled Einstein--Cartan ------------------------
\subsection{Minimally-coupled Einstein--Cartan}%
\label{SymbolicReference}
Three quadratic torsion invariants with couplings $\alpha_{1,2,3}$
are added to Einstein--Maxwell; no curvature-squared or nonminimal
terms appear, and the torsion components enter only in equations
with LHS $=0$ (\cref{ReferenceKineticOutput}); discussed in
\cref{SectorSurvey}.

\subsection{Propagating PGT with the curvature-squared term}%

graviton-perturbation sector to a fourth-order system: in
\cref{PropagatingKineticOutput}, $\mathcal{H}_{xx}$,
$\mathcal{H}_{yy}$, and $\mathcal{H}_{zz}$ each acquire a
$2\,b_{5}\,\partial_t^{4}$ kinetic prefactor on the LHS;

% --- E.5  Ricci--EM nonminimal coupling -----------------------------
\subsection{Ricci--EM nonminimal coupling}%
\label{SymbolicNonminimal}
A single new vertex
$\delta_{1}\tilde{\mathcal{R}}_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}$ is added to the
Einstein--Cartan terms (\cref{NonminimalKineticOutput}). The
$\delta_{1}$ coupling generates entries in every
$\mathcal{H}_{\mu\nu}$ equation and second-time-derivative entries
in the torsion sector; discussed in \cref{SectorSurvey}.

% --- E.6  Full YM--PGT nonminimal -----------------------------------
\subsection{Full YM--PGT nonminimal}%
\label{SymbolicGeneralNonminimal}
The full Yang--Mills-form Poincar\'e gauge Lagrangian turns on
$\beta_{1,2,3}$, $\xi$, $\delta_{1}$, $\chi$, $\zeta_{1,2,3}$
simultaneously. The Bahamonde, Barker, and Shapiro variants of
\cref{SectorSurvey} are parameter restrictions of the same
Lagrangian; the surveyed system is collected in
\cref{GeneralNonminimalKineticOutput}; discussed in
\cref{SectorSurvey}.

% --- E.8  Dark-photon-plasma analogue -------------------------------
\subsection{Dark-photon-plasma analogue}%
\label{SymbolicDarkPhotonPlasma}
A kinetic-mixing vertex $\delta_{m}\,F_{\mu\nu}\tilde{F}^{\mu\nu}$
(with $\tilde{F}$ the torsion-trace field strength) and a photon
mass term $m_{A}^{2}$ are added to the Einstein--Cartan
Lagrangian. $m_{A}^{2}$ enters the diagonal photon
kinetic term; $\delta_{m}$ enters the off-diagonal
photon--torsion entries (\cref{DarkPhotonPlasmaKineticOutput});
discussed in \cref{SectorSurvey}.
