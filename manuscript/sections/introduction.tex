decades in frequency, with nanohertz pulsar-timing
arrays~\cite{nanograv2023fifteen, EPTA:2023fyk, Reardon:2023gzh},

preheating~\cite{Easther:2006gt}, first-order phase
transitions~\cite{Grojean:2006bp}, or coalescence of
primordial black holes~\cite{Dolgov:2012be}.

Several indirect-detection schemes for this band have been
proposed, based on the conversion of gravitational waves to
photons in magnetic fields --- a standard general-relativistic
process known as the Gertsenshtein
effect~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave, boccaletti1970conversion}.
Two routes have been explored: reanalyses of astrophysical
backgrounds in which the galactic or extragalactic magnetic
field acts as the conversion
medium~\cite{lella2024constraining, Domcke:2020yzq}; and
reinterpretations of dark-matter cavity
haloscopes~\cite{ADMX:2025vom, HAYSTAC:2025nrt, Salemi:2019xgl}
as Gertsenshtein-channel
searches~\cite{Berlin:2021txa, Domcke:2022rgu}. No confirmed
Gertsenshtein detection has been reported.

The mechanism is the spin-two analogue of axion--photon
mixing~\cite{raffelt1988mixing}, and has been re-examined in
standard general relativity through exact solutions in a
constant background~\cite{ejlli2020graviton}, perturbative
treatments~\cite{Ejlli:2018hke}, and two-state mixing~\cite{palessandro2023simple, palessandro2024gertsenshtein}.
Hwang and Noh, and Tomomatsu~\etal, have critiqued pervasive flaws
in the standard arguments and identified restricted validity regimes
for the conversion
formula~\cite{hwangnoh2023emdef, hwangnoh2023graviton, hwangnoh2024nonlinear, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein}.

\paragraph*{Astrophysical scales}
The scale of any Gertsenshtein conversion is bounded by
the product of background magnetic-field (BGMF) strength and
propagation length. BGMFs available to
indirect-detection schemes span galactic regular
fields of order $10^{-10}\,\mathrm{T}$ over kiloparsec
baselines~\cite{Domcke:2020yzq, Domcke:2022rgu, lella2024constraining},
and magnetar surface fields of order $10^{11}\,\mathrm{T}$ over
kilometre extents~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq}.
Propagation through cosmological backgrounds does not amplify
this product: Anninos~\etal find that, in FLRW and
de~Sitter universes, the mixing length exceeds the Hubble radius,
and oscillations are damped~\cite{Anninos:2024esx}.
At magnetar fields, the bare conversion factor
is of order $10^{-8}$, so existing proposals for observability
each invoke astrophysical
amplification~\cite{kushwaha2022magnetar, mcdonald2024magnetar, Wu:2026nds};
a candidate setting is the binary neutron star merger /
magnetar coincidence proposed for GW190425 and
FRB~20190425A~\cite{Wu:2026nds}.

\paragraph*{Poincar\'e gauge theory}
The sensitivity of the Gertsenshtein effect to the structure of the
gravitational theory motivates our investigation within
the framework of Poincar\'e gauge theory (PGT).
Torsion and curvature are introduced as independent field strengths, and the
PGT Lagrangian contains a vast, rich landscape of
potential interactions~\cite{sezgin1980ghostfree, Sezgin:1981xs, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, barker2024poincare}.
Torsion propagation is heavily constrained, \eg by the
Yo--Nester theorem ruling out vector-torsion
propagation~\cite{Yo:2001sy}, which leaves only scalar-torsion
ghost- and tachyon-free at linear order.

\paragraph*{Prior work}
The gauge-theoretic completion of Einstein's theory, wherein
the local Poincar\'e symmetry is gauged
on the same footing as the internal symmetries of the Standard
Model, dates to the work of Utiyama, Kibble, Sciama, Hehl, and
others~\cite{utiyama1956invariant, kibble1961lorentz, sciama1962physical, hayashi1979new, trautman1972einstein, hehl1976spin, hehl1995metric, blagojevic2002gravitation}.
The PGT operator landscape was first charted in
the ghost- and tachyon-free analyses of Sezgin--van
Nieuwenhuizen; the modern Hamiltonian classifications of
Lin--Nester and
Blagojevi\'c~\cite{sezgin1980ghostfree, Sezgin:1981xs, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian}.
Aoki~\etal have predicted naturally ghost-free inflationary universes~\cite{Aoki:2020zqm},
and Barker~\etal extend to conformal embeddings that reopen
the vector-torsion
sector~\cite{barker2021hamiltonian, barker2024poincare} for propagation.
Constructive proposals for ghost-free phenomenology include
non-minimal photon--torsion couplings introduced at
Lagrangian
level~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic, rubilar2003torsion, itin2004maxwell};
Weyl-symmetric extensions~\cite{karananas2024weyl, legner2025torc};
and cubic-curvature terms that cancel quadratic ghosts at
higher order~\cite{bahamonde2025cubic}.

\paragraph*{In this paper}
We compute the Gertsenshtein conversion across the quadratic
Poincar\'e gauge action, using \textit{\TIDAL{}}, a
symbolic--numerical software package that derives the linearised
equations of motion directly from the Lagrangian.
We find ghost-free sectors of the quadratic PGT action in which
Gertsenshtein conversion is genuinely amplified relative to the
Einstein--Maxwell baseline, and sectors in which it is suppressed.
