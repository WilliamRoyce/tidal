\section{Introduction}%
\label{Introduction}

\paragraph*{High-frequency gravitational waves and the gauge-gravity sector}
Gravitational-wave observation now spans roughly ten decades in frequency, from the nanohertz pulsar-timing band, where evidence of a stochastic background has now been reported~\cite{nanograv2023fifteen}, through the millihertz reach of LISA~\cite{amaro2017lisa} to the kilohertz audio sensitivity of LIGO--Virgo--KAGRA~\cite{abbott2023gwtc3}. Above a few kilohertz the dedicated instrumentation thins to nothing, leaving the megahertz--gigahertz window observationally empty even as that window covers the source range expected from primordial black-hole mergers, cosmic strings, preheating after inflation, and direct production in the very early Universe~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq, Aggarwal:2025noe}. With no operating interferometer sensitive in this band, a programme of indirect detection has developed that reads out the gravitational signal through its electromagnetic conversion rather than through metric strain, exploiting bulk-acoustic-wave resonators, reinterpreted axion haloscopes, and cosmic-photon-background bounds in galactic and intergalactic magnetic fields~\cite{lella2024constraining, Domcke:2020yzq, Berlin:2021txa, Goryachev:2021zzn, Aggarwal:2025noe}. The simplest such mechanism is the Gertsenshtein effect: the resonant mixing of a gravitational wave with a photon mode in an external magnetic background~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave, boccaletti1970conversion}. Even the general-relativistic baseline of this conversion has been the subject of recent corrections and reanalyses~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton, hwangnoh2023emdef, palessandro2023simple, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein}; and while non-minimal torsion--electromagnetic couplings and torsion-modified light propagation have been studied within Poincar\'e gauge theory~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic, itin2004maxwell, rubilar2003torsion, Kruglov:2008cv, Gaete:2008vb, Gaete:2009dd, obukhov2024electrodynamics, bahamonde2025torsion}, the corresponding graviton--photon conversion calculation in a PGT background carrying propagating torsion has not been performed.

\paragraph*{Torsion theory space and the spin-1 ghost restriction}
The obstacle to such a calculation is twofold. The first is one of scale: the quadratic Poincar\'e gauge Lagrangian defines a multi-parameter operator space whose Gertsenshtein channel structure must be computed sector by sector~\cite{sezgin1980ghostfree, Sezgin:1981xs, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, barker2024poincare}, and manual derivation across this space is infeasible for more than one or two models at a time --- the same scaling argument that has motivated the spectrum-level PGT pipelines~\cite{barker2024psalter, barker2025hamilcar}. The second is a sharp consistency restriction. The canonical Yo--Nester no-go theorem~\cite{Yo:1999ex, Yo:2001sy}, since reproduced and extended by exhaustive Hamiltonian studies~\cite{blagojevic2018hamiltonian, BeltranJimenez:2019hrm, Aoki:2020zqm}, rules out generic vector-torsion propagation in a quadratic PGT background, leaving only the scalar-torsion sector with weakly-coupled, ghost- and tachyon-free linear propagation; recent work has begun to revisit this restriction in scale-invariant extensions of the gauge group~\cite{barker2024poincare}. The Gertsenshtein channel in PGT therefore lives at the intersection of two active questions: which torsion sectors propagate as physical degrees of freedom, and how do those sectors couple to electromagnetism in a background magnetic field.

\paragraph*{The Gertsenshtein channel: prior derivations and their tensions}
Graviton--photon conversion in a static magnetic background is a classical result of general relativity coupled to Maxwell electrodynamics; the closed-form conversion probability has been known since the early 1970s~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave, boccaletti1970conversion, DeLogi:1977qe}, with the mechanism understood as the spin-two analogue of axion--photon mixing~\cite{raffelt1988mixing}. The same result has subsequently been recovered by exact resummation in a constant background~\cite{ejlli2020graviton, Ejlli:2018hke} and by a two-state quantum-mechanical mixing argument~\cite{palessandro2023simple, palessandro2024gertsenshtein}, with constraint analyses based on the inverse process placing upper limits on stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds at ultra-high frequencies~\cite{Ejlli:2019bqj, lella2024constraining}. However, the derivational landscape carries unresolved tensions: recent reanalyses have surfaced inconsistencies in the metric-coupling treatment of the electromagnetic field and in the consistency of the magnetic background with its own gravitational backreaction~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton, hwangnoh2023emdef, hwangnoh2024nonlinear, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein}, motivating the load-bearing numerical validation reported in~\cref{Results}.

\paragraph*{The high-frequency gravitational-wave window and its astrophysical anchors}
The window in which graviton--photon conversion has
observational reach is the high-frequency range above a few
kilohertz, where no operating interferometer is sensitive and
the expected source population --- mergers of light primordial
black holes, cosmic-string cusps and kinks, preheating after
inflation, and direct radiation from very-early-Universe
processes --- lies beyond the Standard
Model~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq, Aggarwal:2025noe}.
Conversion sensitivity in this window grows with the
product of the gravitational coupling, the background
field strength, and the coherent propagation length, so the
astrophysical scales of interest cover many decades: from
galactic regular fields of order $10^{-10}\,\mathrm{T}$ across
kiloparsec baselines~\cite{Domcke:2020yzq, Domcke:2022rgu, lella2024constraining}
to magnetar surface fields of order $10^{11}\,\mathrm{T}$ over
kilometre extents~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq}. The intervening
laboratory regime, in which static magnetic fields of order
ten Tesla can be sustained over metre-scale path lengths, is
many orders of magnitude short of either astrophysical anchor;
the laboratory conversion probability across a single coherent
traversal is correspondingly negligible, and the observational
reach of the channel rests on astrophysical baseline lengths
rather than on controllable field strength. Reanalysis of
cosmic photon backgrounds in galactic and intergalactic
magnetic fields --- treating the radio-through-gamma-ray
spectrum as the upper limit on what an inverse Gertsenshtein
process could have produced --- already constrains the
stochastic high-frequency gravitational-wave spectrum at
$\Omega_{\mathrm{GW}} \lesssim 10^{-6}$ across several decades
of the megahertz-to-exahertz range~\cite{lella2024constraining, He:2023xoh},
while complementary direct-detection proposals based on
bulk-acoustic-wave resonators~\cite{Goryachev:2021zzn, Lasky:2021naa, Campbell:2025mks}
and axion-haloscope reinterpretation~\cite{Berlin:2021txa} now
extend the experimental programme into the same window. Any
correction to the Einstein--Maxwell graviton--photon vertex
that arises from a torsion-carrying gravity theory therefore
enters a phenomenology with established observational
infrastructure and upper limits tight enough to discriminate
order-unity corrections in several decades of the
high-frequency spectrum.

\paragraph*{Poincar\'e gauge theory and adjacent prior work}
The gauge-theoretic completion of general relativity, in which
the local Poincar\'e symmetry of special relativity is gauged
on the same footing as the internal symmetries of the Standard
Model, dates to the work of Utiyama, Kibble, Sciama, and Hehl
and collaborators~\cite{utiyama1956invariant, kibble1961lorentz, sciama1962physical, hehl1976spin, shapiro2002torsion, blagojevicHehl2013}.
Viable subspaces have
been catalogued in a sustained line of recent work~\cite{barker2024poincare, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, Aoki:2020zqm};
constructive directions for ghost-free phenomenology ---
non-minimal $F^2T^2$-type couplings of the photon to
torsion~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic, rubilar2003torsion, itin2004maxwell},
Weyl-symmetric embeddings that restore propagating modes by
enlarging the gauge group~\cite{karananas2024weyl, legner2025torc},
and cubic-curvature extensions that cancel quadratic ghosts at
higher order~\cite{bahamonde2025cubic} --- are developed
in~\cref{Discussion}. The closest prior treatment of
graviton--photon mixing in a modified-gravity background is
the scalar--tensor and $f(R)$ analysis of Cembranos and
collaborators, which generalised the Einstein--Maxwell mixing
kernel to a broad family of metric-only modified-gravity
theories~\cite{Cembranos:2018jxo, Cembranos:2023ere} and which
the present work extends in a direction that family does not
cover. Two further strands of adjacent prior work bear on the
calculation. Torsion--photon coupling in an external magnetic
background has been considered in higher-derivative quantum
gravity~\cite{Kruglov:2008cv} and in path-dependent
gauge-invariant formalisms that treat the photon
classically~\cite{Gaete:2008vb, Gaete:2009dd}; graviton--photon
mixing has separately been generalised to cosmological
backgrounds with stochastic magnetic fields~\cite{Addazi:2024kbq},
to astrophysical blazar-jet environments in standard general
relativity~\cite{Matsuo:2025blj}, and to metric-affine gravity
through projective-symmetry-induced photon couplings~\cite{Janssen:2018exh};
the propagating-mode and polarisation phenomenology of PGT
gravitational waves themselves has been developed in
parallel~\cite{Obukhov:2017pxa, Alves:2023rxs, BeltranJimenez:2019hrm, Katanaev:2020xdv}.
Of the available torsion-carrying gravity frameworks, PGT is
the one in which the connection between gauge structure and
propagating spectrum has been most thoroughly worked out and
in which the ghost-freedom classifications have been carried
furthest~\cite{Yo:1999ex, Yo:2001sy, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, barker2024poincare};
it is therefore the framework in which the Gertsenshtein
channel can be addressed against an existing PGT phenomenology
and stability literature. However, the Gertsenshtein channel
structure in any PGT background carrying propagating torsion
has not previously been computed.

\paragraph*{In this report}
The calculation
is carried out within \TIDAL{}, a symbolic--numerical pipeline
that derives the linearised equations of motion from a
quadratic PGT-plus-Maxwell Lagrangian and integrates them on a
Fourier modal solver auto-selected for flat-background
periodic-domain configurations; like \PSALTer{} and
\Hamilcar{} for spectrum-level PGT analyses~\cite{barker2024psalter, barker2025psalter2, barker2025hamilcar},
\TIDAL{} extends the symbolic--numerical PGT toolchain to the
coupled graviton--photon system. The survey itself works in
the plane-wave and uniform-magnetic-field benchmark standard
in the Gertsenshtein literature. We use
\TIDAL{} to span the PGT operator inventory catalogued
in~\cref{Results}.
