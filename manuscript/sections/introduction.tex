\section{Introduction}%
\label{Introduction}

\paragraph*{High-frequency gravitational waves and the gauge-gravity sector}
Gravitational-wave observation now spans roughly ten decades
in frequency, from the nanohertz pulsar-timing band, where
evidence of a stochastic background has been
reported~\cite{nanograv2023fifteen}, through the millihertz
reach of LISA~\cite{amaro2017lisa} to the kilohertz audio
sensitivity of LIGO--Virgo--KAGRA~\cite{abbott2023gwtc3}.
Above a few kilohertz no operating interferometer is
sensitive, leaving the megahertz--gigahertz window --- the
band of primordial black-hole mergers, cosmic strings,
preheating after inflation, and very-early-Universe
production~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq, Aggarwal:2025noe} ---
observationally empty. Indirect-detection programmes therefore
exploit graviton--photon conversion in cosmic and laboratory
magnetic fields~\cite{lella2024constraining, Domcke:2020yzq, Berlin:2021txa, Goryachev:2021zzn, Aggarwal:2025noe}.
The simplest such mechanism is the Gertsenshtein effect: the
resonant mixing of a gravitational wave with a photon mode in
an external magnetic
background~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave, boccaletti1970conversion}.
Even the general-relativistic baseline has been corrected and
reanalysed in recent
work~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton, hwangnoh2023emdef, palessandro2023simple, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein};
and while non-minimal torsion--electromagnetic couplings and
torsion-modified light propagation have been studied within
Poincar\'e gauge theory~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic, itin2004maxwell, rubilar2003torsion, Kruglov:2008cv, Gaete:2008vb, Gaete:2009dd, obukhov2024electrodynamics, bahamonde2025torsion},
the corresponding graviton--photon conversion calculation in
a PGT background carrying propagating torsion has not been
performed.

\paragraph*{Torsion theory space and the spin-1 ghost restriction}
The quadratic Poincar\'e gauge Lagrangian defines a
multi-parameter operator space whose Gertsenshtein channel
structure must be computed sector by
sector~\cite{sezgin1980ghostfree, Sezgin:1981xs, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, barker2024poincare};
the dimensionality of this space, together with the
requirement that any candidate calculation recover the
Einstein--Maxwell Boccaletti result as a benchmark, sets the
scope of any survey-level treatment. Within that space, a
sharp consistency restriction further constrains where
torsion is allowed to propagate at all: the canonical
Yo--Nester no-go theorem~\cite{Yo:1999ex, Yo:2001sy}, since
reproduced and extended by exhaustive Hamiltonian
studies~\cite{blagojevic2018hamiltonian, BeltranJimenez:2019hrm, Aoki:2020zqm},
rules out generic vector-torsion propagation in a quadratic
PGT background, leaving only the scalar-torsion sector with
weakly-coupled, ghost- and tachyon-free linear propagation;
recent work has begun to revisit this restriction in
scale-invariant extensions of the gauge
group~\cite{barker2024poincare}. The Gertsenshtein channel in
PGT therefore lives at the intersection of two active
questions: which torsion sectors propagate as physical
degrees of freedom, and how do those sectors couple to
electromagnetism in a background magnetic field.

\paragraph*{The Gertsenshtein channel: prior derivations and their tensions}
Graviton--photon conversion in a static magnetic background
is a classical result of general relativity coupled to Maxwell
electrodynamics; the closed-form conversion probability dates
from the early
1970s~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave, boccaletti1970conversion, DeLogi:1977qe},
with the mechanism understood as the spin-two analogue of
axion--photon mixing~\cite{raffelt1988mixing}. Subsequent work
has rederived this result by exact resummation in a constant
background~\cite{ejlli2020graviton, Ejlli:2018hke} and by a
two-state mixing
argument~\cite{palessandro2023simple, palessandro2024gertsenshtein},
and inverted it to bound stochastic gravitational-wave
backgrounds at ultra-high
frequencies~\cite{Ejlli:2019bqj, lella2024constraining}.
However, the derivational landscape carries unresolved
tensions: recent reanalyses have surfaced inconsistencies in
the metric-coupling treatment of the electromagnetic field and
in the consistency of the magnetic background with its own
gravitational backreaction~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton, hwangnoh2023emdef, hwangnoh2024nonlinear, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein},
motivating the numerical validation we report
in~\cref{Results}.

\paragraph*{Astrophysical anchors}
The product of background field strength and coherent
propagation length sets the observational reach of any
Gertsenshtein-class measurement, and the scales of interest
cover many decades: from galactic regular fields of order
$10^{-10}\,\mathrm{T}$ over kiloparsec
baselines~\cite{Domcke:2020yzq, Domcke:2022rgu, lella2024constraining}
to magnetar surface fields of order $10^{11}\,\mathrm{T}$ over
kilometre extents~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq}. Reanalyses of
cosmic photon backgrounds --- treating the
radio-through-gamma-ray spectrum as an upper limit on what an
inverse Gertsenshtein process could have produced --- already
constrain the stochastic high-frequency gravitational-wave
spectrum at $\Omega_{\mathrm{GW}} \lesssim 10^{-6}$ across
several decades of the megahertz-to-exahertz
range~\cite{lella2024constraining, He:2023xoh}.

\paragraph*{Poincar\'e gauge theory and adjacent prior work}
The gauge-theoretic completion of general relativity, in which
the local Poincar\'e symmetry of special relativity is gauged
on the same footing as the internal symmetries of the Standard
Model, dates to the work of Utiyama, Kibble, Sciama, and Hehl
and collaborators~\cite{utiyama1956invariant, kibble1961lorentz, sciama1962physical, hehl1976spin, shapiro2002torsion, blagojevicHehl2013}.
Viable subspaces have
been catalogued in a sustained line of recent work~\cite{barker2024poincare, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, Aoki:2020zqm};
constructive directions for ghost-free phenomenology ---
non-minimal $F^2T^2$-type couplings of the photon to
torsion~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic, rubilar2003torsion, itin2004maxwell},
Weyl-symmetric embeddings that restore propagating modes by
enlarging the gauge group~\cite{karananas2024weyl, legner2025torc},
and cubic-curvature extensions that cancel quadratic ghosts at
higher order~\cite{bahamonde2025cubic} --- are developed
in~\cref{Discussion}. The closest prior treatment of
graviton--photon mixing in a modified-gravity background is
the scalar--tensor and $f(R)$ analysis of Cembranos and
collaborators, which generalised the Einstein--Maxwell mixing
kernel to a broad family of metric-only modified-gravity
theories~\cite{Cembranos:2018jxo, Cembranos:2023ere} and which
the present work extends in a direction that family does not
cover. Two further strands of adjacent prior work bear on the
calculation. Torsion--photon coupling in an external magnetic
background has been considered in higher-derivative quantum
gravity~\cite{Kruglov:2008cv} and in path-dependent
gauge-invariant formalisms that treat the photon
classically~\cite{Gaete:2008vb, Gaete:2009dd}; graviton--photon
mixing has separately been generalised to cosmological
backgrounds with stochastic magnetic fields~\cite{Addazi:2024kbq},
to astrophysical blazar-jet environments in standard general
relativity~\cite{Matsuo:2025blj}, and to metric-affine gravity
through projective-symmetry-induced photon couplings~\cite{Janssen:2018exh};
the propagating-mode and polarisation phenomenology of PGT
gravitational waves themselves has been developed in
parallel~\cite{Obukhov:2017pxa, Alves:2023rxs, BeltranJimenez:2019hrm, Katanaev:2020xdv}.
However, the Gertsenshtein channel structure in any PGT
background carrying propagating torsion has not previously
been computed.

\paragraph*{In this report}
In this work we derive the linearised graviton--photon mixing
kernel for the general quadratic Poincar\'e gauge theory
coupled to Maxwell electrodynamics, and show that the kinetic
matrix decouples the hx and ax gravitational-wave polarisations
from the torsion fields at linear order in $\Bzero$,
independently of the quadratic coupling
constants~(\cref{HxAxIdentity}). The calculation is carried
out within \TIDAL{}, a symbolic--numerical pipeline that
derives the linearised equations of motion from a quadratic
PGT-plus-Maxwell
Lagrangian~\cite{barker2024psalter, barker2025psalter2, barker2025hamilcar}.
We use \TIDAL{} to span the PGT operator inventory catalogued
in~\cref{Results}, working in the plane-wave and
uniform-magnetic-field benchmark standard in the Gertsenshtein
literature.
