\section{Introduction}%
\label{Introduction}

\paragraph*{High-frequency gravitational waves and the gauge-gravity sector}
Gravitational-wave observation now extends across twelve
decades in frequency, with principal operating instruments in
the nanohertz pulsar-timing band~\cite{nanograv2023fifteen},
the kilohertz Advanced LIGO~\cite{LIGOScientific:2014pky},
Advanced Virgo~\cite{VIRGO:2014yos}, and KAGRA~\cite{KAGRA:2020tym}
network, and the planned millihertz reach of LISA~\cite{amaro2017lisa}.

The megahertz--gigahertz band lies above the upper frequency
of any known astrophysical source~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq, Aggarwal:2025noe},
so any source in this window must be of primordial or
beyond-Standard-Model origin --- for instance inflationary
preheating, first-order phase transitions, or mergers of light
primordial black holes.

Several indirect-detection schemes have been proposed or
operated for this band, exploiting the conversion of
gravitational waves to photons in magnetic fields --- a
standard general-relativistic process known as the
Gertsenshtein effect~\cite{gertsenshtein1962wave, boccaletti1970conversion}
--- either in astrophysical magnetic-field environments through
cosmic photon-background reanalyses~\cite{lella2024constraining, Domcke:2020yzq}
or in laboratory apparatus where a known magnetic field acts
as the conversion medium~\cite{Berlin:2021txa, Domcke:2022rgu, Goryachev:2021zzn};
no confirmed detection has been reported.

For Einstein--Maxwell the conversion probability grows with
the magnetic-field strength and the coherent propagation

baselines, and any modified-gravity calculation must be
assessed against this benchmark.

mixing~\cite{raffelt1988mixing}, and has been re-examined in

formulations~\cite{palessandro2023simple, palessandro2024gertsenshtein},
and corrections to the metric-coupling treatment of the
electromagnetic field~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton, hwangnoh2023emdef, hwangnoh2024nonlinear, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein}.

\paragraph*{Torsion theory space and the spin-1 ghost restriction}
The quadratic Poincar\'e gauge Lagrangian carries a
multi-parameter operator space of admissible torsion--curvature
couplings~\cite{sezgin1980ghostfree, Sezgin:1981xs, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, barker2024poincare},
and torsion propagation within that space is sharply
restricted.

%     PGT classification.
%   lin2019pgt + blagojevic2018hamiltonian: modern Hamiltonian

The standard no-go theorem on vector-torsion
propagation~\cite{Yo:1999ex, Yo:2001sy}, since reproduced and
extended by exhaustive Hamiltonian studies~\cite{blagojevic2018hamiltonian, BeltranJimenez:2019hrm, Aoki:2020zqm},
rules out generic vector-torsion propagation in a quadratic
PGT background, leaving only the scalar-torsion sector ghost-
and tachyon-free at linear order.

This restriction has recently been reopened by the observation
that scale-invariant extensions of the gauge group naturally
introduce a Maxwell-type kinetic term for vector torsion,
restoring its propagating character within an extended Weyl
framework~\cite{barker2024poincare}.

therefore depends both on the form of the
torsion--electromagnetic coupling and on the propagating-mode
content of the chosen PGT sector.

\paragraph*{Astrophysical scales and observational bounds}
The reach of any Gertsenshtein-class measurement is bounded by
the product of background field strength and coherent
propagation length, and the scales of interest span many
decades: galactic regular fields of order
$10^{-10}\,\mathrm{T}$ over kiloparsec
baselines~\cite{Domcke:2020yzq, Domcke:2022rgu, lella2024constraining}
and magnetar surface fields of order $10^{11}\,\mathrm{T}$ over
kilometre extents~\cite{Aggarwal:2020olq} bracket the regimes
available to indirect-detection schemes.
At magnetar fields the bare graviton--photon conversion factor
is of order $10^{-8}$, so observability requires an additional
astrophysical amplification stage~\cite{kushwaha2022magnetar, mcdonald2024magnetar, tomomatsu2025gertsenshtein, Wu:2026nds};
a candidate setting is the binary-neutron-star-merger /
magnetar coincidence proposed for GW190425 and
FRB~20190425A~\cite{Wu:2026nds}.

\paragraph*{Poincar\'e gauge theory and adjacent prior work}
The gauge-theoretic completion of general relativity, in which
the local Poincar\'e symmetry of special relativity is gauged
on the same footing as the internal symmetries of the Standard
Model, dates to the work of Utiyama, Kibble, Sciama, and Hehl
and collaborators~\cite{utiyama1956invariant, kibble1961lorentz, sciama1962physical, hehl1976spin, shapiro2002torsion, blagojevicHehl2013}.
Viable subspaces have been catalogued in a sustained line of
recent work~\cite{barker2024poincare, lin2019pgt, blagojevic2018hamiltonian, Aoki:2020zqm};
constructive directions for ghost-free phenomenology include
non-minimal $F^2T^2$-type couplings of the photon to
torsion~\cite{hehl2000electromagnetic, rubilar2003torsion, itin2004maxwell},
Weyl-symmetric embeddings that restore propagating modes by
enlarging the gauge group~\cite{karananas2024weyl, legner2025torc},
and cubic-curvature extensions that cancel quadratic ghosts at
higher order~\cite{bahamonde2025cubic}.

The closest prior treatment of graviton--photon mixing in a
modified-gravity background is the scalar--tensor and $f(R)$
extension of Cembranos and
collaborators~\cite{Cembranos:2018jxo, Cembranos:2023ere},
which the present PGT calculation extends to theories with
dynamical torsion, beyond the metric-only frameworks those
papers cover.
% Cembranos sentence reworded from v7 awkward "along the torsion
%   direction those metric-only frameworks cannot reach"

Two further strands of adjacent prior work bear on the
calculation. Torsion--photon coupling in magnetic backgrounds
has been considered in higher-derivative quantum
gravity~\cite{Kruglov:2008cv} and in path-dependent
gauge-invariant formalisms~\cite{Gaete:2008vb, Gaete:2009dd}.
Graviton--photon mixing has been generalised to cosmological
backgrounds with stochastic magnetic fields~\cite{Addazi:2024kbq},
to astrophysical blazar-jet environments~\cite{Matsuo:2025blj},
and to metric-affine gravity via projective-symmetry-induced
photon couplings~\cite{Janssen:2018exh}. PGT
gravitational-wave propagation and polarisation phenomenology
has been developed in
parallel~\cite{Obukhov:2017pxa, Alves:2023rxs, BeltranJimenez:2019hrm, Katanaev:2020xdv}.

%   sentence is split into three short sentences per round-8

However, the Gertsenshtein channel structure in any PGT
background carrying propagating torsion has not previously
been computed.

\paragraph*{In this report}
In this work we derive the linearised graviton--photon mixing
kernel for the general quadratic Poincar\'e gauge theory
coupled to Maxwell electrodynamics, and show that the kinetic
matrix decouples the hx and ax gravitational-wave polarisations
from the torsion fields at linear order in $\Bzero$,
independently of the quadratic coupling
constants~(\cref{HxAxIdentity}). The calculation is carried
out within \TIDAL{}, a symbolic--numerical pipeline that
derives the linearised equations of motion from a quadratic
PGT-plus-Maxwell
Lagrangian~\cite{barker2024psalter, barker2025psalter2, barker2025hamilcar}.
We use \TIDAL{} to span the PGT operator inventory catalogued
in~\cref{Results}, working in the plane-wave and
uniform-magnetic-field benchmark standard in the Gertsenshtein
literature.
