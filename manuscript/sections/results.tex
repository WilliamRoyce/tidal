\section{Results}%
\label{Results}

% Opening: BHL Pattern A (recall the action) + B (state the object of

% quantities (\Aamp from App G, A(θ) from App J) used throughout §4.
To survey the Gertsenshtein conversion across the Poincar\'{e} gauge
theory of \cref{QuadraticAction} we partition the general quadratic
action by sector and probe each restriction in turn. The operator
inventory across the parity-even and parity-odd quadratic,
derivative-extension, cubic, and Chern--Simons sectors is collected
in \cref{MasterParameterCount}; the perturbative-regime conditions
under which the linearised treatment remains self-consistent are set
out in \cref{PerturbativeRegime}; and the nested-sampling layer with
which we infer per-sector amplification or suppression is detailed in
\cref{InferenceArchitecture}. Throughout, the per-sample conversion
score $A(\theta)$ of \cref{AmplificationScore} drives the Bayesian
likelihood and anchors the dimensionless comparison against the
Einstein--Maxwell baseline established in \cref{ValidationResult}
below; the small-$\Bzero$ asymptotic ratio $\Aamp$ of
\cref{AmplificationFactor} additionally applies to the plane-wave
uniform-$\Bzero$ benchmark, where the Taylor coefficient
$C_0$ of \cref{SurveyCoefficient} is extractable. A
non-propagating-torsion control test (\cref{InformativeAmplification})
disambiguates the amplification mechanism against the simplest
propagating-torsion instability alternative.

\subsection{Validation against the Einstein--Maxwell baseline}%
\label{ValidationResult}
% Three named paragraphs: setup, Boccaletti reproduction, survey
% observables.  The survey-observables paragraph carries the App G + App J
% amplification-metric cross-refs that anchor the per-theory verdicts in
% §4.2–§4.4.

\paragraph*{Validation setup}
We anchor the survey on a reproduction of the Boccaletti conversion
kernel \cref{GertsenshteinFormula} in pure Einstein--Maxwell, the
regime in which the formula was originally derived. The validation
sweep evolves the linearised system on a periodic $N = 512$ grid
covering a propagation distance $D = 100$ in natural units, with
horizon $t_\text{end} = 50$ and photon carrier wavenumber
$k = 2.011$. We scan a 24-point grid in $\Bzero \in [0.005, 0.25]$
and integrate using the Fourier modal backend, the primary numerical
innovation underlying every result that follows (\cref{ModalSolver}).

\paragraph*{Reproduction of the Boccaletti kernel}
\TIDAL{} recovers the Boccaletti kernel \cref{GertsenshteinFormula}
in the small-$\Bzero$ limit, reproducing the analytic conversion
probability $\sin^2(\kappa\Bzero t/2)$ to the modal-evolution
round-off floor of $\sim 10^{-8}$ across the validation grid
(\cref{BoccalettiValidation}, \cref{BoccalettiUniform}). As
$\Bzero$ grows the simulation departs from the bare formula by
$\mathcal{O}(\Bzero^2)$, capturing the graviton effective-mass
detuning that the bare formula drops but the full PDE retains;
against the two-mode prediction of~\cite{raffelt1988mixing} that
includes this physics, the simulation residual returns to the
round-off floor across the sweep, confirming that the bare-formula
deviation reflects retained physics rather than numerical error.
The path-integrated kernel for a Gaussian magnetic profile of
finite extent is recovered to the same precision
(\cref{BoccalettiLocalised}), and the energy-conservation audit
across every theory class considered in this work is collected in
\cref{ConservationAudit}.

\paragraph*{Survey observables}
The validation also fixes the per-sample conversion score reported
throughout \cref{SurveySummary,InformativeAmplification,FrontierAndNulls}.
At each posterior sample $\theta$ the inference layer evaluates the
finite-$\Bzero$, finite-$t$ score
$A(\theta) = \Pmax(\theta)/\sin^2(\kappa\Bzero t/2)$ of
\cref{AmplificationScore}, which recovers $A=1$ on the
Einstein--Maxwell baseline and serves as the nested-sampling
likelihood. The asymptotic small-$\Bzero$ ratio $\Aamp$ of
\cref{AmplificationFactor} additionally provides a
$\Bzero$-independent figure of merit when the Taylor coefficient
$C_0$ of \cref{SurveyCoefficient} is extractable; this asymptotic
form applies in the plane-wave uniform-$\Bzero$ benchmark on which
the survey below is built but is not generally applicable to the
finite-extent geometry that occupies the discussion in
\cref{Discussion}.

Under the plane-wave uniform-$\Bzero$ benchmark we obtain the
partition of the surveyed PGT hierarchy by Bayes factor collected in
\cref{SurveyResultsTable}: of the thirteen sectors traversed at this
benchmark, seven show informative posteriors favouring amplification
($\log B = \log\mathcal{Z}_+ - \log\mathcal{Z}_- > 0$), one
(the parity-odd minimal sub-sector) shows a clean suppression
preference, two of the highest-dimensional parity-odd sectors are
geometrically incompatible with the plane-wave ansatz at any viable
horizon (eigenvalues exceed the modal-solver overflow ceiling
\cref{NestedScore}), one parallel QED-EFT axis is a clean null, and
the Einstein--Cartan baseline reduces analytically to the
Einstein--Maxwell limit. Bayes factors across the amplification-favouring
sectors range from $\log B \approx +2$ in the Ricci--electromagnetism
cross-term to $\log B \approx +13$ in the complete parity-even sector
after the full $\chi$-derivative basis is closed; the
non-propagating-torsion control retains $\log B \approx +8$ when the
torsion kinetic mode is removed, supporting the genuine-channel-mixing
interpretation over a propagating-torsion-instability artefact (the
definitive disambiguation defers to the finite-extent geometry of
\cref{Discussion}). Whether the surviving amplification preferences
reflect genuine amplification of the conversion channel or unbounded
growth on the tachyonic plane-wave background that a finite-extent
geometry would saturate is a methodological distinction that occupies
the discussion in \cref{Discussion}. \Cref{InformativeAmplification}
walks through the per-sector posteriors that exhibit the
amplification preference and \cref{FrontierAndNulls} reports the
analytic null, the suppression preference, the parity-odd
geometric incompatibility, and the QED-EFT parallel-axis null.

\subsection{Informative amplification under the plane-wave benchmark}%
\label{InformativeAmplification}%
\label{ChannelStructure}% Compatibility carry-over for theory.tex line 377 cross-ref.

The six sectors with informative posteriors share a common structural
feature visible in the overlaid corner plots
\cref{CornerT1,CornerT4,CornerT5D20,CornerT5D21,CornerT5D22,CornerT5D23}:
the amplification-rewarding posterior concentrates in a sub-region of
coupling space distinct from the suppression-rewarding posterior, and
the prior-weighted overlap is sufficiently small that the paired Bayes
factor reaches at least one order of magnitude. The amplification
corner is reached by a specific combination of couplings rather than
a coordinate direction in any one variable, and the dominant
direction migrates progressively as the non-minimal coupling space
is extended.

\paragraph*{The dark-photon-plasma sector}
The dark-photon-plasma extension promotes the trace-vector torsion
$\T[2]{_\mu}$ to a Proca field with kinetic mixing to the photon,
recovering the standard dark-photon phenomenology
of~\cite{caputo2021darkphoton} at $\Bzero \to 0$,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{dp}
    &= -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
      + \Bet{3} I_3
      - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}} \nonumber \\
    &\quad - \tfrac{1}{2}m_A^2\,\T[2]{_\mu}\T[2]{^\mu}
      &&\text{[Proca mass]} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \xi\,\T[2]{_\mu}\PD{_\nu}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}
      &&\text{[kinetic mixing]} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \delta_m\,\T[2]{_\mu}\T[2]{^\mu}\,
              \FieldF{_{\alpha\beta}}\FieldF{^{\alpha\beta}}
      &&\text{[plasma detuning]} \, ,
  \label{DarkPhotonPlasmaLagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over
$\{m_A^2, \xi, \delta_m, \Bet{3}\}$. The positive-definite
kinetic-mixing parameter $\xi$ and Proca mass $m_A^2$ are sampled
from log-uniform priors over six decades; the sign-symmetric detuning
$\delta_m$ uses the arctan-uniform construction of
\cref{InferenceArchitecture}. The two equivalent formulations of this
Lagrangian agree to the modal-evolution round-off floor
(\cref{ProcaVTEquivalence}), so the survey result is
formulation-independent. The publication-quality chain returns
$\log\mathcal{Z}_+ = +9.03 \pm 0.07$ against
$\log\mathcal{Z}_- = +2.92 \pm 0.08$ ($\log B = +6.11$) under the
plane-wave benchmark.

\paragraph*{The Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term sector}
The Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term sector retains the
$\Del{1}\MAGFt{_{[\mu\nu]}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}$ coupling of
\cref{ParityEvenQuadraticAction} as the only operator beyond the
linear and torsion-squared sectors,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{rf}
    &= -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
      + \Bet{1} I_1 + \Bet{2} I_2 + \Bet{3} I_3
      - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \Del{1}\,\MAGFt{_{[\mu\nu]}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}
      &&\text{[Ricci--EM cross-term]} \, ,
  \label{RicciEMLagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over
$\{\Bet{1}, \Bet{2}, \Bet{3}, \Del{1}\}$. The cross-term sources the
linearised conversion amplitude directly at order $\Bzero$, providing
a $\Del{1}$-linear correction to the Gertsenshtein channel that does
not require torsion to propagate; the sign-symmetric $\Del{1}$ is
sampled from \cref{InferenceArchitecture}. The publication-quality
chain returns $\log\mathcal{Z}_+ = +13.37 \pm 0.06$ against
$\log\mathcal{Z}_- = +11.40 \pm 0.10$ ($\log B = +1.98$) under the
plane-wave benchmark. The overlaid posterior of \cref{CornerT4}
resolves the dominant direction: amplification regions extend
broadly in $\Del{1}$ at large $\Alp{3}$, while suppression regions
are tightly localised at $\Del{1} \approx 0$ and small $\Alp{i}$.

\paragraph*{The Yang--Mills--PGT non-minimal progression}
We extend the non-minimal parity-even sector progressively across
four nested sub-theories and probe the corresponding nested
Bayes-factor progression. The Bahamonde base retains the parity-even
torsion-squared invariants together with a positive-definite kinetic
operator $\xi (\nabla\mathcal{T})^2$ and the Ricci--EM cross-term
$\Del{1}$; the Barker extension adds the single derivative-mixing
parameter $\chi$; the Shapiro extension instead adds the three
derivative couplings $\zeta_{1,2,3}$; and the full non-minimal sector
takes the nine-parameter union
$\{\Bet{1\!-\!3}, \xi, \Del{1}, \chi, \zeta_{1\!-\!3}\}$,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{nm}
    &= \lag_\text{rf}
      - \tfrac{1}{4}\xi\,
              \mathcal{F}^{(T)}_{\alpha\beta}\,
              \mathcal{F}^{(T)\,\alpha\beta}
      &&\text{[Barker torsion kinetic]} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \chi\,\MAGFt{_{[\alpha\beta]}}\,
              \mathcal{F}^{(T)\,\alpha\beta}
      &&\text{[Barker derivative mixing]} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \zeta_1\,\rcD{_\alpha}\mathcal{T}^{\beta}{}_{\beta\gamma}\,
              \FieldF{^{\alpha\gamma}}
      &&\text{[Shapiro 1]} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \zeta_2\,\rcD{_\alpha}\mathcal{T}^{\alpha}{}_{\beta\gamma}\,
              \FieldF{^{\beta\gamma}}
      &&\text{[Shapiro 2]} \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \zeta_3\,\rcD{_\alpha}\mathcal{T}_{\beta}{}^{\alpha}{}_{\gamma}\,
              \FieldF{^{\beta\gamma}}
      &&\text{[Shapiro 3]} \, ,
  \label{NonMinimalLagrangian}
\end{align}
The Shapiro non-minimal derivative class to which $\chi$ and
$\zeta_i$ belong is enumerated in \cref{DerivativeExtensions}.
Across the four sub-theories the Bayes factor grows from
$\log B = +1.84$ at the Bahamonde base to $\log B = +7.37$ at the
full non-minimal union, a monotone strengthening of the
amplification preference under the plane-wave benchmark as the
non-minimal coupling space is extended: each additional class of
coupling opens a further amplification corner in the prior. The
corresponding overlaid posteriors in
\cref{CornerT5D20,CornerT5D21,CornerT5D22,CornerT5D23} resolve the
direction in which each new coupling deposits its amplification
signal: in the Bahamonde base it is the $\Del{1}$--$\xi$ corner; in
the Barker extension the additional $\chi$ direction broadens the
amplification posterior into a non-axis-aligned region; in the
Shapiro extension the $\zeta_i$ couplings tighten the suppression
sub-region near $\zeta_i \approx 0$; and the full non-minimal sector
accumulates these features. Only Barker's single $\chi$ is included
in the derivative class probed here; the full $\xi_1$--$\xi_{16}$
ghost-free kinetic basis~\cite{barker2024poincare} is closed
separately in the kinetic-closure sector of \cref{FrontierAndNulls}.

\subsection{Frontier nulls under the plane-wave benchmark}%
\label{FrontierAndNulls}

Four sectors at the frontier of the survey resolve to nulls under
the plane-wave benchmark for structurally distinct reasons: the
$\Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2$ quadratic-curvature sector decouples analytically
from the transverse-traceless graviton--photon channel; the
parity-odd minimal sub-sector $\{\Bet{1\!-\!3}, d_{21}\}$ exhibits a
clean suppression preference; the full and complete parity-odd
sectors are geometrically incompatible with the plane-wave
uniform-$\Bzero$ ansatz at any viable horizon; and a parallel
QED-EFT survey axis along the Euler--Heisenberg $F^4$ corrections is
a clean null at the surveyed field strengths.

\paragraph*{The quadratic-curvature sector (R$^2$-PGT)}
The R$^2$-PGT extension retains a single curvature-squared invariant
on top of the Einstein--Cartan baseline,
\begin{align}
  \lag_{\mathrm{R^2}}
    &= -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
       + \Bet{1} I_1 + \Bet{2} I_2 + \Bet{3} I_3
       - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\alpha\beta}}\FieldF{^{\alpha\beta}}
       \nonumber \\
    &\quad + \Alp{1}\,\MAGFt{}\,\MAGFt{}
       &&\text{[Ricci-scalar squared]}\, ,
  \label{R2PGTLagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over $\{\Alp{1}, \Bet{1}, \Bet{2},
\Bet{3}\}$. The $\Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2$ term is the only
$\Bzero$-independent curvature contribution: its expansion to quadratic
order in the perturbation \cref{PerturbationAnsatz} sources from the
Ricci scalar of the Riemann--Cartan connection, which in turn contains
torsion-field contributions through the contortion split
\cref{PalatiniDecomposition}. Under the plane-wave transverse-traceless
graviton initial condition on the uniform $\Bzero$ background, the
torsion-field components of $\MAGFt{}$ vanish identically at zeroth
order, so the leading $\Alp{1}$ correction to the conversion
probability is suppressed by a factor of the torsion amplitude squared
relative to the Boccaletti kernel. The amplification score $A(\theta)$
is therefore identically unity at $\Alp{1}$-leading order across the
full $\Alp{1}$ prior, independent of the $\Alp{1}$ numerical value;
the symbolic kinetic matrix of \cref{PropagatingKineticOutput} exhibits
the decoupling explicitly as a vanishing off-diagonal entry coupling
$\Alp{1}$ to the $h_\times \!\leftrightarrow\! a_\times$ channel.

\paragraph*{The Einstein--Cartan baseline}
