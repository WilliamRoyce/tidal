\section{Results}%
\label{Results}

% Opening: BHL Pattern A (recall the action) + B (state the object of

To survey the Gertsenshtein conversion across the Poincar\'{e} gauge
theory of \cref{QuadraticAction} we partition the general quadratic
action by sector and probe each restriction in turn. The operator
inventory across the parity-even and parity-odd quadratic, derivative-
extension, cubic-completion, and Chern--Simons sectors is collected
in \cref{MasterParameterCount} (App~\ref{PGTEnumeration}); the
perturbative-regime conditions under which the linearised treatment
remains self-consistent are set out in \cref{PerturbativeRegime}
(App~\ref{PerturbativeRegime}); and the nested-sampling layer with
which we infer per-sector amplification or suppression is detailed
in \cref{InferenceArchitecture} (App~\ref{InferenceArchitecture}).
Throughout, the per-sample conversion score $A(\theta)$ of
\cref{AmplificationScore} drives the Bayesian likelihood and the
small-$\Bzero$ amplification factor $\Aamp$ of
\cref{AmplificationFactor} anchors the dimensionless comparison
against the Einstein--Maxwell baseline established in
\cref{ValidationResult} below.

\subsection{Validation against the Einstein--Maxwell baseline}%
\label{ValidationResult}
% Three named paragraphs: setup, Boccaletti reproduction, survey
% observables.  The survey-observables paragraph carries the App G + App J
% amplification-metric cross-refs that anchor the per-theory verdicts in
% §4.2–§4.4.

\paragraph*{Validation setup}
We anchor the survey on a reproduction of the Boccaletti conversion
kernel \cref{GertsenshteinFormula} in pure Einstein--Maxwell, the
regime in which the formula was originally derived. The validation
sweep evolves the linearised system on a periodic $N = 512$ grid
covering a propagation distance $D = 100$ in natural units, with
horizon $t_\text{end} = 50$, photon carrier wavenumber $k = 2.011$,
and gravitational coupling $\kappa = 1$. We scan a 24-point grid in
$\Bzero \in [0.005, 0.25]$ --- spanning the small-coupling regime in
which the bare Boccaletti formula is valid through to the regime
where the Raffelt--Stodolsky effective-mass detuning becomes
visible --- and integrate using the Fourier modal backend, the
primary numerical innovation underlying every result that follows
(\cref{ModalSolver}).

\paragraph*{Reproduction of the Boccaletti kernel}
\TIDAL{} reproduces the Boccaletti kernel as expected. Across the
swept $\Bzero$ range the simulation residual against the bare formula
of \cref{GertsenshteinFormula} grows with $\Bzero^2$, peaking at
$|P_\text{TIDAL} - P_\text{bare}| \lesssim 2.4 \times 10^{-3}$ at
the high-$\Bzero$ end of the sweep, where the graviton acquires an
effective mass and the two-mode formula of
Raffelt--Stodolsky~\cite{raffelt1988mixing} supersedes the bare
formula. Against the two-mode prediction the same simulation sits
at the modal-evolution round-off floor of $\sim 10^{-8}$ across the
full sweep, demonstrating that the truncation in the bare formula
--- not in our integration --- accounts for the entire residual;
the full overlay and the matching $(\Delta/g)^4$ scaling are
collected in \cref{BoccalettiUniform}, and the localised-$\Bzero$
counterpart, in which the wavepacket traverses a Gaussian magnetic
profile of finite extent, recovers the path-integrated
kernel~\cite{raffelt1988mixing} to the same precision
(\cref{BoccalettiLocalised}). The energy-conservation audit across
every theory class considered in this survey is collected
in~\cref{ConservationAudit}.

\paragraph*{Survey observables}
The validation also fixes the two amplification quantities reported
throughout \cref{SurveySummary,InformativeAmplification,FrontierAndNulls}.
At each posterior sample $\theta$ the inference layer evaluates the
finite-$\Bzero$, finite-$t$ score
$A(\theta) = \Pmax(\theta)/\sin^2(\kappa\Bzero t/2)$ of
\cref{AmplificationScore}, which recovers $A=1$ on the
Einstein--Maxwell baseline and serves as the nested-sampling
likelihood. Where the small-$\Bzero$ Taylor coefficient $C_0$ of
\cref{SurveyCoefficient} can be extracted from the chain, the
asymptotic survey-coefficient ratio $\Aamp$ of
\cref{AmplificationFactor} provides the $\Bzero$-independent
amplification figure of merit that strips the $\Bzero^4$ corrections
of \cref{PerturbativityHierarchy}; the two quantities agree to
$\mathcal{O}(\Bzero^2)$ in the validation regime.

\subsection{Survey summary under the plane-wave benchmark}%
\label{SurveySummary}%
\label{SectorSurvey}% Compatibility carry-over for theory.tex / App~D / App~F / App~G legacy cross-refs.

Under the plane-wave uniform-$\Bzero$ benchmark we obtain the
partition of the surveyed PGT hierarchy by Bayes factor collected in
\cref{SurveyResultsTable}: of the ten sectors traversed at this
benchmark, six show informative posteriors favouring amplification
($\log B = \log\mathcal{Z}_+ - \log\mathcal{Z}_- > 0$), one
(the parity-odd minimal sub-sector) shows a clean suppression
preference, and three of the highest-dimensional frontier sectors
collapse to the soft-penalty floor of the likelihood
\cref{NestedScore}, indicating geometric incompatibility of the
parity-odd and complete-PGT operator sets with the plane-wave benchmark
rather than a numerical pathology. The amplification preferences range
from a modest $\approx 7{:}1$ in the Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term
to $\approx 1600{:}1$ in the full nine-dimensional Yang--Mills--PGT
non-minimal scan, with the trend strengthening monotonically as the
non-minimal coupling space is expanded. \Cref{InformativeAmplification}
walks through the per-sector posteriors that exhibit the amplification
preference and \cref{FrontierAndNulls} reports the analytic and
geometric nulls.

\subsection{Informative amplification under the plane-wave benchmark}%
\label{InformativeAmplification}%
\label{ChannelStructure}% Compatibility carry-over for theory.tex line 377 cross-ref.

The six sectors with informative posteriors share a common structural
feature visible in the overlaid corner plots
\cref{CornerT1,CornerT4,CornerT5D20,CornerT5D21,CornerT5D22,CornerT5D23}:
the amplification-rewarding posterior concentrates in a sub-region of
coupling space distinct from the suppression-rewarding posterior, and
the prior-weighted overlap is sufficiently small that the paired Bayes
factor reaches at least one order of magnitude. The amplification
corner is reached by a specific combination of couplings rather than
a coordinate direction in any one variable, and the dominant
direction migrates progressively as the non-minimal coupling space
is extended.

\paragraph*{Dark-photon plasma (T1)}
The dark-photon-plasma extension promotes the trace-vector torsion
$\T[2]{_\mu}$ to a Proca field with kinetic mixing to the photon,
recovering the standard dark-photon phenomenology
of~\cite{caputo2021darkphoton} at $\Bzero \to 0$,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T1}
    = -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
    + \Bet{3} I_3
    - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}
    - \tfrac{1}{2}m_A^2\,\T[2]{_\mu}\T[2]{^\mu}
    + \xi\,\T[2]{_\mu}\PD{_\nu}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}
    + \delta_m\,\T[2]{_\mu}\T[2]{^\mu}\,\FieldF{}^2,
  \label{T1Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over $\{m_A^2, \xi, \delta_m, \Bet{3}\}$.
The positive-definite kinetic-mixing parameter $\xi$ and Proca mass
$m_A^2$ are sampled from log-uniform priors over six decades; the
sign-symmetric detuning $\delta_m$ uses \cref{InferenceArchitecture}. The
fundamental-vector and TorsionCDT formulations agree to the
modal-evolution round-off floor under the equivalence $m_T^2 = 2\Alp{3}$
(\cref{ProcaVTEquivalence}), so the survey is formulation-independent.
The publication-quality chain returns $\log\mathcal{Z}_+ = +9.03 \pm 0.07$
against $\log\mathcal{Z}_- = +2.92 \pm 0.08$, a Bayes factor of
$\log B = +6.11$ corresponding to a $\sim 450{:}1$ preference for the
amplification model.
\emph{Plasma-mediated dark-photon mixing breaks the
Holdom-trivialisation of the conversion channel in the amplification
direction under the plane-wave benchmark.}

\paragraph*{Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term (T4)}
The Ricci--EM cross-term retains the
$\Del{1}\MAGFt{_{[\mu\nu]}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}$ coupling of
\cref{ParityEvenQuadraticAction} as the only operator beyond the
linear and torsion-squared sectors,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T4}
    = -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
    + \Alp{1} I_1 + \Alp{2} I_2 + \Alp{3} I_3
    - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}
    + \Del{1}\,\MAGFt{_{[\mu\nu]}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}},
  \label{T4Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over
$\{\Alp{1}, \Alp{2}, \Alp{3}, \Del{1}\}$. The cross-term sources the
linearised conversion amplitude directly at order $\Bzero$, providing
a $\Del{1}$-linear correction to the Gertsenshtein channel that does
not require torsion to propagate; the sign-symmetric $\Del{1}$ is
sampled from \cref{InferenceArchitecture}. The publication-quality D1 chain
returns $\log\mathcal{Z}_+ = +13.37 \pm 0.06$ against
$\log\mathcal{Z}_- = +11.40 \pm 0.10$, a Bayes factor of
$\log B = +1.98$ ($\approx 7{:}1$ preference for amplification). The
overlaid posterior of \cref{CornerT4} resolves the dominant direction:
amplification regions extend broadly in $\Del{1}$ at large $\Alp{3}$,
while suppression regions are tightly localised at $\Del{1} \approx 0$
and small $\Alp{i}$.
\emph{The Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term opens an
amplifying vertex at linear order in $\Bzero$ under the plane-wave
benchmark, with $\Del{1}$ as the dominant direction.}

\paragraph*{Yang--Mills--PGT non-minimal progression (T5)}
We extend the non-minimal parity-even sector progressively across four
nested sub-theories (T5.0--T5.3) and probe the corresponding nested
Bayes-factor progression. The base sub-theory T5.0 (after Bahamonde et
al.) retains the parity-even torsion-squared invariants together with
a positive-definite kinetic operator $\xi (\nabla\mathcal{T})^2$ and
the Ricci--EM cross-term $\Del{1}$, while subsequent sub-theories add
Barker's single derivative-mixing parameter $\chi$ (T5.1), the three
Shapiro derivative couplings $\zeta_{1,2,3}$ (T5.2 omits $\chi$), and
the full nine-parameter union $\{\Bet{1\!-\!3}, \xi, \Del{1}, \chi,
\zeta_{1\!-\!3}\}$ (T5.3):
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T5}
    = \lag_\text{T4}\bigl|_{\Alp{i}\to\Bet{i}}
    + \xi\,(\nabla\mathcal{T})^2
    + \chi\,\mathcal{T}\,\nabla\mathcal{F}
    + \sum_{i=1}^{3}\zeta_i\,\mathcal{T}^{(i)}\,\mathcal{F}\,\nabla\mathcal{F}.
  \label{T5Lagrangian}
\end{align}
The Shapiro non-minimal derivative class to which $\chi$ and $\zeta_i$
belong is enumerated in \cref{DerivativeExtensions}. Across the four
sub-theories the Bayes factor grows from $\log B = +1.84$ at the
five-dimensional base to $\log B = +7.37$ at the full nine-dimensional
union, a monotone strengthening of the amplification preference as the
non-minimal coupling space is extended: each additional class of
coupling (the Barker $\chi$, the Shapiro $\zeta_i$) opens a further
amplification corner in the prior. The corresponding overlaid posteriors
in \cref{CornerT5D20,CornerT5D21,CornerT5D22,CornerT5D23} resolve the
direction in which each new coupling deposits its amplification signal:
in T5.0 it is the $\Del{1}$--$\xi$ corner, in T5.1 the additional $\chi$
direction broadens the amplification posterior into a non-axis-aligned
region, in T5.2 the $\zeta_i$ couplings tighten the suppression sub-region
near $\zeta_i \approx 0$, and in T5.3 the union accumulates these
features. Only Barker's single $\chi$ is included in the derivative
class probed here; the full $\xi_1$--$\xi_{16}$ ghost-free kinetic
basis~\cite{barker2024poincare} and the parity-even derivative-extension
sector enumerated in \cref{DerivativeExtensions} remain partial.
\emph{Across the Yang--Mills--PGT non-minimal progression the amplification
preference strengthens monotonically with non-minimal coupling space
under the plane-wave benchmark, reaching $\log B = +7.37$ at the full
nine-dimensional union.}

\subsection{Frontier nulls under the plane-wave benchmark}%
\label{FrontierAndNulls}

Three sectors at the frontier of the survey resolve to nulls under
the plane-wave benchmark for structurally distinct reasons: the
$\Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2$ (R$^2$-PGT) sector decouples analytically from the
transverse-traceless graviton--photon channel; the parity-odd minimal
sub-sector $\{\Bet{1\!-\!3}, d_{21}\}$ exhibits a clean suppression
preference; and the full parity-odd (T6) and complete parity-even
(T7) sectors collapse to the soft-penalty floor of the likelihood,
reflecting geometric incompatibility of the plane-wave + uniform-$\Bzero$
geometry with the parity-odd and higher-derivative operator classes.

\paragraph*{Quadratic curvature: R$^2$-PGT (T3)}
The R$^2$-PGT extension retains a single curvature-squared invariant
on top of the Einstein--Cartan baseline,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T3}
    = -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
    + \Alp{1}\,\MAGFt{}^2
    + \Bet{1} I_1 + \Bet{2} I_2 + \Bet{3} I_3
    - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}},
  \label{T3Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over $\{\Alp{1}, \Bet{1}, \Bet{2},
\Bet{3}\}$. The $\Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2$ term is the only
$\Bzero$-independent curvature contribution: its expansion to quadratic
order in the perturbation \cref{PerturbationAnsatz} sources from the
Ricci scalar of the Riemann--Cartan connection, which in turn contains
torsion-field contributions through the contortion split
\cref{PalatiniDecomposition}. Under the plane-wave transverse-traceless
graviton initial condition on the uniform $\Bzero$ background, the
torsion-field components of $\MAGFt{}$ vanish identically at zeroth
order, so the leading $\Alp{1}$ correction to the conversion
probability is suppressed by a factor of the torsion amplitude squared
relative to the Boccaletti kernel. The amplification score $A(\theta)$
is therefore identically unity at $\Alp{1}$-leading order across the
full $\Alp{1}$ prior, independent of the $\Alp{1}$ numerical value;
the symbolic kinetic matrix of \cref{PropagatingKineticOutput} exhibits
the decoupling explicitly as a vanishing off-diagonal entry coupling
$\Alp{1}$ to the $h_\times \!\leftrightarrow\! a_\times$ channel.
\emph{The $\Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2$ curvature-squared sector decouples
analytically from the transverse-traceless Gertsenshtein channel under
the plane-wave benchmark.}

\paragraph*{Einstein--Cartan minimal (T2)}
The minimal Einstein--Cartan baseline retains the three parity-even
torsion-squared couplings $\{\Bet{1}, \Bet{2}, \Bet{3}\}$ together
with the Einstein--Hilbert kinetic term and the Maxwell term; the
auxiliary-torsion algebraic constraint inherited from variation against
the Riemann--Cartan connection reduces the linearised plane-wave
Gertsenshtein channel to its Einstein--Maxwell baseline at all
$\{\Bet{i}\}$, so the sector is not separately scanned in the present
benchmark. The symbolic kinetic matrix at this parameter point is
archived in \cref{ReferenceKineticOutput} and serves as the reference
against which all per-class kinetic-matrix outputs are compared.
\emph{Einstein--Cartan minimal acts as the auxiliary-torsion reference
baseline for the survey rather than a separately scanned theory.}

\paragraph*{Geometric nulls: full T6, T7, and T9}
The full parity-odd (T6), complete parity-even (T7), and full
kinetic-closure (T9) sectors collapse to the soft-penalty floor of the
likelihood \cref{NestedScore}, with 100\% (T6), 74\% (T7), and 100\%
(T9) of dead points at $\log L \approx -100$. T6 retains the twenty
parity-odd couplings of \cref{ChernSimonsAction} and
\cref{ParityOddQuadratic} on top of the T5 base; T7 replaces Barker's
single $\chi$ with the full ten-parameter $\chi_1$--$\chi_{10}$ basis
of the parity-even $\tilde{\mathcal{R}} \times \nabla\mathcal{T}$ class
on top of T5; T9 further replaces Barker's single $\xi$ with the full
sixteen-parameter $\xi_1$--$\xi_{16}$ kinetic basis of the
$(\nabla\mathcal{T})^2$ class (one invariant, $\xi_{11}$, vanishes in
the plane-wave reduction, leaving fifteen propagating). All three
sectors return $\log B \approx 0$ to within numerical error
($+0.10 \pm 0.03$ for T6, $-0.01 \pm 0.04$ for T7, $-0.02 \pm 0.04$
for T9), consistent with posteriors dominated by the soft-floor noise
band rather than physical amplification or suppression. The structural
origin of the null is geometric: the stable region of the plane-wave
+ uniform-$\Bzero$ configuration in the parity-odd coupling space has
measure zero in the continuous prior (the $d_{19}=d_{20}=d_{21}=0$
stable submanifold for T6 and the analogous higher-derivative
submanifolds for T7 and T9 are not positive-measure under
arctan-uniform sampling), so the nested sampler cannot accumulate
posterior weight in the physical-amplification or physical-suppression
regions; the soft-penalty floor of \cref{NestedScore} captures the
remaining samples that traverse tachyonic-divergence configurations
on the plane-wave background. The corresponding overlaid posteriors of
\cref{CornerT6Full,CornerT7,CornerT9} show the floor-bound structure
visually: both amplification and suppression posteriors are nearly
identical and centred on the prior median in every panel. T9
specifically closes the $\xi_1$--$\xi_{16}$ kinetic sector beyond
Barker's single $\xi$ ($= \mathrm{LC}\{\xi_6, \xi_7, \xi_{12},
\xi_{16}\}$), so the kinetic-completion null is structural rather than
a scope artefact.
\emph{The full parity-odd, complete parity-even, and full
kinetic-closure sectors of the PGT hierarchy are geometrically
incompatible with the plane-wave uniform-$\Bzero$ benchmark; the
resulting nulls reflect the geometry of the benchmark, not the
structure of the theories.}

\paragraph*{Surveyed-sector scope}
The plane-wave benchmark presented here covers the full parity-even
quadratic operator inventory of \cref{ParityEvenQuadraticAction} (T5
through T9), the Chern--Simons quadratic and Nieh--Yan invariants of
\cref{ChernSimonsAction} (T6), the three Shapiro non-minimal derivative
couplings $\zeta_{1,2,3}$ of \cref{DerivativeExtensions}, the full
ten-parameter $\chi_1$--$\chi_{10}$ $\tilde{\mathcal{R}}\times\nabla\mathcal{T}$ basis
(T7), and the full sixteen-parameter $\xi_1$--$\xi_{16}$ kinetic basis
(T9, with $\xi_{11}$ vanishing in plane-wave reduction). The complete
parity-odd derivative-extension class
($\varepsilon\cdot(\nabla\mathcal{T})^2$, 38 invariants), the
parity-odd complete closure $xt_{1\text{--}36}$ of T8, and the
cubic-completion sector of \cref{CubicCompletions} lie outside the
surveyed scope of the present benchmark.
