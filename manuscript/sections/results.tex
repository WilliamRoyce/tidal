\section{Results}%
\label{Results}

To survey the Gertsenshtein conversion across the Poincar\'{e} gauge
theory of \cref{QuadraticAction} we partition the general quadratic
action by sector and probe each restriction in turn. The operator
inventory across the parity-even and parity-odd quadratic, derivative-
extension, cubic-completion, and Chern--Simons sectors is collected
in \cref{MasterParameterCount} (App~\ref{PGTEnumeration}); the
perturbative-regime conditions under which the linearised treatment
remains self-consistent are set out in \cref{PerturbativeRegime}
(App~\ref{PerturbativeRegime}); and the nested-sampling layer with
which we infer per-sector amplification or suppression is detailed
in \cref{InferenceArchitecture} (App~\ref{InferenceArchitecture}).
Throughout, the per-sample conversion score $A(\theta)$ of
\cref{AmplificationScore} drives the Bayesian likelihood and the
small-$\Bzero$ amplification factor $\Aamp$ of
\cref{AmplificationFactor} anchors the dimensionless comparison
against the Einstein--Maxwell baseline established in
\cref{ValidationResult} below.

\subsection{Validation against the Einstein--Maxwell baseline}%
\label{ValidationResult}
% Draftable now: data local at benchmark_results/canonical/boccaletti_uniform_rich.json,
% figure already rendered at manuscript/figures/fig1_boccaletti_validation.pdf.
% Three named paragraphs: setup, Boccaletti reproduction, survey
% observables.  The survey-observables paragraph carries the App G + App J
% amplification-metric cross-refs that anchor the per-theory verdicts in
% §4.2–§4.4.

\paragraph*{Validation setup}
We anchor the survey on a reproduction of the Boccaletti conversion
kernel \cref{GertsenshteinFormula} in pure Einstein--Maxwell, the
regime in which the formula was originally derived. The validation
sweep evolves the linearised system on a periodic $N = 512$ grid
covering a propagation distance $D = 100$ in natural units, with
horizon $t_\text{end} = 50$, photon carrier wavenumber $k = 2.011$,
and gravitational coupling $\kappa = 1$. We scan a 24-point grid in
$\Bzero \in [0.005, 0.25]$ --- spanning the small-coupling regime in
which the bare Boccaletti formula is valid through to the regime
where the Raffelt--Stodolsky effective-mass detuning becomes
visible --- and integrate using the Fourier modal backend, the
primary numerical innovation underlying every result that follows
(\cref{ModalSolver}).

\paragraph*{Reproduction of the Boccaletti kernel}
\TIDAL{} reproduces the Boccaletti kernel as expected. Across the
swept $\Bzero$ range the simulation residual against the bare formula
of \cref{GertsenshteinFormula} grows with $\Bzero^2$, peaking at
$|P_\text{TIDAL} - P_\text{bare}| \lesssim 2.4 \times 10^{-3}$ at
the high-$\Bzero$ end of the sweep, where the graviton acquires an
effective mass and the two-mode formula of
Raffelt--Stodolsky~\cite{raffelt1988mixing} supersedes the bare
formula. Against the two-mode prediction the same simulation sits
at the modal-evolution round-off floor of $\sim 10^{-8}$ across the
full sweep, demonstrating that the truncation in the bare formula
--- not in our integration --- accounts for the entire residual;
the full overlay and the matching $(\Delta/g)^4$ scaling are
collected in \cref{BoccalettiUniform}, and the localised-$\Bzero$
counterpart, in which the wavepacket traverses a Gaussian magnetic
profile of finite extent, recovers the path-integrated
kernel~\cite{raffelt1988mixing} to the same precision
(\cref{BoccalettiLocalised}). The energy-conservation audit across
every theory class considered in this survey is collected
in~\cref{ConservationAudit}.

\paragraph*{Survey observables}
The validation also fixes the two amplification quantities reported
throughout \cref{MinimalCouplingSector,NonMinimalParityEvenSector,ParityOddSector}.
At each posterior sample $\theta$ the inference layer evaluates the
finite-$\Bzero$, finite-$t$ score
$A(\theta) = \Pmax(\theta)/\sin^2(\kappa\Bzero t/2)$ of
\cref{AmplificationScore}, which recovers $A=1$ on the
Einstein--Maxwell baseline and serves as the nested-sampling
likelihood. Where the small-$\Bzero$ Taylor coefficient $C_0$ of
\cref{SurveyCoefficient} can be extracted from the chain, the
asymptotic survey-coefficient ratio $\Aamp$ of
\cref{AmplificationFactor} provides the $\Bzero$-independent
amplification figure of merit that strips the $\Bzero^4$ corrections
of \cref{PerturbativityHierarchy}; the two quantities agree to
$\mathcal{O}(\Bzero^2)$ in the validation regime.

\label{MinimalCouplingSector}%

In the minimal-coupling sector only the linear sector
(\cref{LinearAction}) and the parity-even torsion-squared invariants
$\{I_1, I_2, I_3\}$ of \cref{TorsionDirectBasis} are active: no
curvature-squared kinetic operators promote torsion to a propagating
field. The torsion sector remains auxiliary in the Einstein--Cartan
sense and the Gertsenshtein conversion proceeds through the metric
channel alone, with torsion entering only via its algebraic source
in the linearised Cartan equation. The full operator inventory of
this sector is the $\{\Bet{1}, \Bet{2}, \Bet{3}\}$ row of
\cref{MasterParameterCount}; we probe two extensions, the
Einstein--Cartan baseline at zero electromagnetic mass and the
dark-photon-plasma extension that promotes the trace-vector torsion
to a Proca field at finite mass.

\paragraph*{Einstein--Cartan baseline}
The Einstein--Cartan baseline retains the three parity-even
torsion-squared couplings of \cref{TorsionDirectBasis} together with
the Einstein--Hilbert kinetic term,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T2}
    = -\frac{1}{\kappa^2}\,\MAGFt{}
    + \Bet{1} I_1 + \Bet{2} I_2 + \Bet{3} I_3
    - \tfrac{1}{4}\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}},
  \label{T2Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a three-dimensional prior over
$\{\Bet{1}, \Bet{2}, \Bet{3}\}$. The couplings are sign-symmetric
dimensionless invariants of the torsion-squared sector, so we sample
each from the arctan-uniform prior of \cref{ArctanPrior}, the
Cauchy-like density of which compactifies the full real line while
concentrating posterior weight near the origin. The symbolic kinetic
matrix at this parameter point is archived in
\cref{ReferenceKineticOutput} and serves as the reference against
which all per-class kinetic-matrix outputs are compared.

\paragraph*{Dark-photon-plasma extension}
The dark-photon-plasma extension adds a Proca mass to the trace-vector
torsion $\T[2]{_\mu}$ and a kinetic-mixing parameter $\xi$ between
$\T[2]{_\mu}$ and the photon, recovering the standard dark-photon
phenomenology of~\cite{caputo2021darkphoton} at $\Bzero\to 0$,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T1}
    = \lag_\text{T2}
    - \tfrac{1}{2}m_A^2\,\T[2]{_\mu}\T[2]{^\mu}
    + \xi\,\T[2]{_\mu}\PD{_\nu}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}
    + \delta_m\,\T[2]{_\mu}\T[2]{^\mu}\FieldF{}^2,
  \label{T1Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over $\{m_A^2, \xi, \delta_m, \Bet{3}\}$.
The positive-definite mass and kinetic-mixing coefficients
$\{m_A^2, \xi\}$ are sampled from log-uniform priors over six decades;
the sign-symmetric detuning $\delta_m$ from \cref{ArctanPrior}. The
fundamental-vector and TorsionCDT formulations of this Lagrangian agree
to the modal-evolution round-off floor under the equivalence
$m_T^2 = 2\Alp{3}$ (\cref{ProcaVTEquivalence}), so the survey results
below are formulation-independent.

\label{ChannelStructure}%
At least one parity-even operator beyond the linear sector is active
in this family: a curvature-squared kinetic term that promotes torsion
to a propagating field, a curvature--electromagnetic cross-term that
opens a new vertex at linear order in $\Bzero$, or a Shapiro
non-minimal derivative coupling that mixes torsion gradients with
$\FieldF{_{\mu\nu}}$. The full operator inventory comes from the
$\{\Alp{1\!-\!6}, \Bet{1\!-\!3}, \Del{1}\}$ row of
\cref{MasterParameterCount} together with the Shapiro non-minimal
derivative class of \cref{DerivativeExtensions}; the symbolic kinetic
matrix in the propagating-torsion regime is archived in
\cref{PropagatingKineticOutput}.

\paragraph*{Quadratic curvature: R$^2$-PGT}
The R$^2$-PGT extension retains a single curvature-squared invariant
on top of the Einstein--Cartan baseline,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T3}
    = \lag_\text{T2}
    + \Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2,
  \label{T3Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving a four-dimensional prior over
$\{\Alp{1}, \Bet{1}, \Bet{2}, \Bet{3}\}$. The $\Alp{1}\MAGFt{}^2$
term is the only $\Bzero$-independent curvature contribution: its
expansion to quadratic order in the perturbation
\cref{PerturbationAnsatz} sources from the Ricci scalar of the
Riemann--Cartan connection, which in turn contains torsion-field
contributions through the contortion split
\cref{PalatiniDecomposition}. Under the plane-wave transverse-traceless
graviton initial condition on the uniform $\Bzero$ background, however,
the torsion-field components of $\MAGFt{}$ vanish identically at zeroth
order, so the leading $\Alp{1}$ correction to the conversion
probability is suppressed by a factor of the torsion amplitude squared
relative to the Boccaletti kernel. The amplification score $A(\theta)$
is therefore identically unity at $\Alp{1}$-leading order across the
full $\Alp{1}$ prior, independent of the $\Alp{1}$ numerical value;
the symbolic kinetic matrix of \cref{PropagatingKineticOutput} exhibits
the decoupling explicitly as a vanishing off-diagonal entry coupling
$\Alp{1}$ to the $h_\times \!\leftrightarrow\! a_\times$ channel.

\paragraph*{Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term}
The Ricci--electromagnetism cross-term retains the
$\Del{1}\MAGFt{_{[\mu\nu]}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}}$ coupling of
\cref{ParityEvenQuadraticAction} as the only operator beyond the
linear and torsion-squared sectors,
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T4}
    = \lag_\text{T2}
    + \Del{1}\,\MAGFt{_{[\mu\nu]}}\FieldF{^{\mu\nu}},

$\{\Del{1}, \Bet{1}, \Bet{2}, \Bet{3}\}$. The cross-term sources

that does not require torsion to propagate; the sign-symmetric
$\Del{1}$ is sampled from \cref{ArctanPrior}.

\paragraph*{Yang--Mills--PGT non-minimal}

\label{ParityOddSector}

The parity-odd sector includes one or more of the Chern--Simons-type
quadratic invariants of \cref{ChernSimonsAction}, the Nieh--Yan
topological density of \cref{LinearAction} promoted to a dynamical
operator under torsion, or the parity-odd derivative extensions of
\cref{ParityOddQuadratic}. These couplings do not block-diagonalise
under the standard transverse-traceless--axial decomposition of the
linearised perturbations, so the Gertsenshtein channel sees the
parity-odd vertices directly at linear order in $\Bzero$ --- a
regime structurally different from the parity-even sectors of
\cref{NonMinimalParityEvenSector}. The full operator inventory comes
from the $\{d_{14\!-\!17}\}$ Chern--Simons row and the parity-odd
derivative-extension rows of \cref{MasterParameterCount}.

\paragraph*{Chern--Simons torsion--electromagnetism coupling}
The Chern--Simons torsion--electromagnetism coupling retains the four
parity-odd quadratic invariants of \cref{ChernSimonsAction} on top of
the parity-even non-minimal base \cref{T5Lagrangian},
\begin{align}
  \lag_\text{T6}
    = \lag_\text{T5}
    + \sum_{i=14}^{17}\,d_i\,\mathcal{X}^{\text{CS}}_{i},
  \label{T6Lagrangian}
\end{align}
giving in total a twenty-two-dimensional prior. All four Chern--Simons
couplings $d_{14\!-\!17}$ are sign-symmetric pseudoscalar invariants
and are sampled from \cref{ArctanPrior}; the parity-odd derivative
extensions are subsumed into the same arctan-uniform family for the
present scan. The high dimensionality of the parity-odd sector is
the motivating context for the sphere-projection joint-prior
construction set out in \cref{CubedSphereAtlas}: a product-of-marginals
prior in twenty-two dimensions allocates exponentially-suppressed
sample weight to non-axis-aligned amplification corners that a unit-norm
direction scan reaches efficiently.
