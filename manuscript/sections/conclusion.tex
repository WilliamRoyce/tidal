\section{Conclusions}%
\label{Conclusions}

In the extensions of general relativity that gravitational-wave
observatories will probe in the coming decade, the action acquires many
new operators with free coupling constants; the Gertsenshtein channel
structure across this coupling space has, until now, been mapped only
model by model. We present \TIDAL{}, a symbolic--numerical pipeline
that automates the linearised conversion analysis for any quadratic
Lagrangian, and apply it to the quadratic Poincar\'{e}-gauge action
coupled to electromagnetism.

Across the surveyed quadratic operator space, a small set of Lagrangian
couplings carries the Gertsenshtein amplification verdict. The
derivative-mixing coupling $\chi_1$ emerges as the dominant amplifying
direction in each of the three parity-even sectors that contain it:
the propagating $\chi$-closure, its non-propagating-torsion control,
and the extended-kinetic-closure embedding. The EM--Riemann--Cartan
curvature coupling $\Del{1}$ plays the same role inside its own
four-dimensional sub-sector. Significant potential for suppression of
conversion also exists across most of the surveyed theories. The
amplification persists in the non-propagating-torsion control with the
torsion kinetic operator pinned to zero, so it depends only on the
non-minimal coupling content; and because no kinetic operator enters
that control at all, ghost-driven growth cannot explain it.

Whether the exponential mode growth that drives these amplification
verdicts represents physical amplification, a tachyonic instability of
the unbounded plane-wave embedding, or a wrong-sign kinetic ghost is
not decided at the linearised Bayesian level. A Hamiltonian
particle-spectrum analysis via
\PSALTer{}~\cite{barker2024psalter,barker2025psalter2} would fix the verdict for
each surviving amplifying direction.

Two natural extensions follow. Re-running the survey on a finite-extent
magnetic background would give the propagating wave only a finite
proper time over which to interact with the field; the exponential
growth that compounds indefinitely under the unbounded plane-wave
embedding is then capped at a finite envelope set by the size of the
magnetised region, the parity-odd full and complete sectors that
overflow under that embedding re-enter the survey with finite-amplitude
posteriors, and coupling-level verdicts would be replaced by magnitude
estimates that admit comparison with detector sensitivities.
Atlas-based direction sampling complements the axis-aligned coupling
scans of the present survey by sampling along directions in the
coupling space rather than along the arbitrarily-chosen named-coupling
axes, identifying which linear combinations of operators in the
surveyed basis carry the amplification and suppression verdicts.

The non-minimal coupling sector of the quadratic Poincar\'{e}-gauge
action is, on the present evidence, the structural carrier of the
linearised Gertsenshtein channel mixing --- a role the
propagating-torsion kinetic sector contributes to the spectrum of the
theory but does not, in itself, supply. Whether each amplifying
coupling identified here drives physical amplification or a
vacuum-dependent instability is a question the linearised survey
cannot, by construction, decide; what it does decide is which operators
of the quadratic gauge-gravity action are even candidates for that
question.
