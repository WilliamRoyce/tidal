\section{Conclusions}%
\label{Conclusions}

The extensions of general relativity that gravitational-wave observatories
will probe in the coming decade proliferate a multi-dimensional coupling
space whose Gertsenshtein channel structure has, until now, been
accessible only one Lagrangian at a time. This thesis develops
\TIDAL{}, a symbolic--numerical pipeline that automates the linearised
channel computation for any quadratic gauge-gravity Lagrangian, and
applies it to the quadratic Poincar\'{e}-gauge + electromagnetic action
as a Bayesian survey at the coupling level.

Across the surveyed quadratic operator space, a small set of named
Lagrangian couplings carries the Gertsenshtein amplification verdict.
The non-minimal EM--Riemann--Cartan curvature coupling $\Del{1}$ and the
derivative-mixing coupling $\chi_1$ emerge as the load-bearing amplifying
directions, robust across the propagating, non-propagating-torsion, and
$\xi$-kinetic-closure embeddings; the parity-odd minimal sub-sector
$\{\Bet{1\!-\!3}, d_{21}\}$ is the only sector that suppresses.
Crucially, the amplification does not require torsion to propagate: the
non-propagating-torsion controls reproduce the amplification corner at
the same coupling content, so the structural ingredient is the
non-minimal coupling itself, not the propagating-torsion kinetic sector.
The amplifying configurations are ghost-free at the linear-order
Hamiltonian level.

Whether the exponential mode growth that drives the amplification
verdicts represents a physical amplification, a non-ghost tachyonic
instability of the unbounded plane-wave embedding, or a wrong-sign
kinetic ghost is not resolved at the linearised Bayesian-survey level
alone. The natural structural route to that resolution is a Hamiltonian
particle-spectrum analysis of the surviving amplifying directions via
\PSALTer{}~\cite{barker2024psalter,barker2025psalter2}. Integration of
\TIDAL{}'s coupling-level identifications with the \PSALTer{} no-ghost
/ no-tachyon classification at each $\Del{1}$, $\chi_1$ point of
interest is the analytic counterpart of the operational extensions
outlined below.

Two extensions are necessary to convert the structural verdicts of the
present survey into physical predictions. The localised-geometry
re-run, on the dual-Gaussian + wavepacket background already supported
by the inference architecture, bounds the interaction time, saturates
the linearised exponential growth without removing the channel mixing,
restores the parity-odd full and complete sectors that overflow under
the plane-wave embedding, and replaces coupling-level verdicts with
magnitude estimates that are physically comparable with detector
sensitivities. The atlas-based direction-sampling machinery, also
already in place, extends the survey from axis-aligned coupling scans
to the full operator space, localising any non-trivial channel
structure that a coarse scan would miss. Both extensions sit inside the
existing \TIDAL{} architecture and are the natural input to the next
phase of the programme.

The non-minimal coupling sector of the quadratic Poincar\'{e}-gauge
action is, on the present evidence, the structural carrier of the
linearised Gertsenshtein channel mixing --- a role the
propagating-torsion kinetic sector contributes to the spectrum of the
theory but does not, in itself, supply. Whether each amplifying
coupling identified here drives physical amplification or a
vacuum-dependent instability is a question the linearised survey
cannot, by construction, decide; what it does decide is which operators
of the quadratic gauge-gravity action are even candidates for that
question.
