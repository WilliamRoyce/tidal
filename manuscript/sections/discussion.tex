\subsection{Discussion and outlook}%
\label{Discussion}

\paragraph*{Interpretation of exponential mode growth}
The amplification ratios themselves grow with the simulation duration,
because the exponential growth compounds indefinitely
on the unbounded plane-wave background.
Physical-magnitude statements about conversion efficiency require
finite-extent, localised-$\Bzero$ simulations that bound the
interaction time and saturate the linearised growth.
We accept the exponentially-growing modes: prejudging which
mode behaviours are unphysical is not the role of a theory-agnostic
software. The
remaining interpretive question is whether each growth represents a
physical amplification, an artifact of the uniform unbounded
background, or a pathology of the Lagrangian.

Several precedents in the
literature show that linearised exponential or tachyonic modes need
not be fatal pathologies. In the Standard Model the Higgs field
appears tachyonic about the unbroken vacuum but is perfectly unitary
about its symmetry-broken ground state~\cite{legner2025torc}.
In the TorC model~\cite{legner2025torc} the linearised
tachyons around $\MAGT{} = 0$ are benign because the physical ground state
is a torsion-condensed phase at $\MAGT{^2} \sim \MPl^2$.
Linearised exponential modes do not by themselves disqualify
a sector; but we do not claim that our surveyed Lagrangians
necessarily realise such a condensation, only that our observations
are not necessarily a pathological.
% the choice of
% vacuum around which the modes are evaluated can change the unitarity
% verdict.

Higher-derivative gravity theories that exhibit Ostrogradsky ghosts at
the linearised level can be non-linearly ghost-free when appropriate
torsion or higher-curvature terms are
present~\cite{aoki2020nonlinear,bahamonde2025cubic}.
Recent analysis~\cite{barker2025irfound1,barker2025irfound2}
argues that a no-ghost-no-tachyon condition that is imposed by hand
on couplings is not stable against radiative
corrections. However,
when the absence of a ghost mode follows from a gauge symmetry of the
Lagrangian itself, the unitarity is protected.
% The non-propagating-torsion
% control of \cref{NonPropagatingControl} is a particularly clean
% example of the latter: with $\xi$ pinned to zero there is no new
% kinetic operator at all, so no wrong-sign coefficient can appear in
% the first place.
This supports the interpretation that the exponential growth we observe is not
automatically fatal.

\paragraph*{Solver overflow}
The sectors in \cref{ParityOddGeomIncomp,XiKineticClosure} yield
eigenvalues so large that the matrix-exponential overflow
ceiling is hit at essentially any accessible propagation time.
A finite interaction time, set by a localised
BGMF, would bound the growth at and restore a
finite-amplitude posterior. The same finite-extent programme would also probe
physics inaccessible to the current setup, including $k$-mode
interactions and realistic geometries that allow physical-magnitude amplification
estimates for actual astrophysical events.

\paragraph*{The $\chi_1$ direction}
It is surprising that the $\chi_1\,\rcD{^\alpha}\MAGT{^{\beta\gamma\delta}}\,
\MAGFt{_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}}$ coupling is always the dominant
amplification distinction whenever present, regardless of
whether the torsion sector propagates. The privilege of this
contraction over the other $\chi_{2\!-\!10}$
and the $\xi_i$ kinetic invariants requires an analysis
of the structure of the conversion equations that is not
considered here.

\paragraph*{Scope and limitations}
The results sit in the linearised regime at first order in $\Bzero$
on an unbounded BGMF with a plane-wave graviton initial
condition. The largest theories in the survey produced
linearised eigenvalues too extreme for the uniform plane-wave setup
to resolve: a substantial fraction of sample points hit modal-solver
overflow in the largest sectors, and resolving them requires the
localised-geometry programme. Interpretation of the exponential growth
may require a \PSALTer{} particle-spectrum analysis.
The cubic-PGT sector has not been surveyed.
