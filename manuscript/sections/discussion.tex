\section{Concluding remarks}
\label{sec:ConcludingRemarks}

This section should be short.
Resist the urge to use florid language such as ``we have shown conclusively that\ldots''.
Instead, be brief and direct.
You may summarise the main results in a few sentences, and then discuss their implications.

You should generally use the passive voice, or `we', in scientific writing.
Avoid the use of `I'.
For example, ``we have shown that\ldots'' is preferable to ``I have shown that\ldots''.
