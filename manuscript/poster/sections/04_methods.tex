\begin{block}{2.\quad Methods}
  \TIDAL{} automates every step from Lagrangian to result, so the vast coupling
  space becomes surveyable:
  \vspace{1ex}
  \begin{center}
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{pipeline_schematic.pdf}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{0.5ex}
  Starting from a \emph{Lagrangian}, \TIDAL{} derives the linearised equations of
  motion symbolically (\xAct{}), evolves them with a spectral solver, and
  uses Bayesian nested sampling to map the informative regions of the coupling space where the
  conversion is modified.
\end{block}
