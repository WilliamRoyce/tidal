\begin{alertblock}{Spacetime torsion can strongly amplify the conversion of
gravitational waves into photons}
  The Gertsenshtein effect --- gravitational waves turning into photons in a
  magnetic field --- is far too weak in general relativity to ever observe, so any
  detectable signal demands physics beyond it. Surveying the operator space of
  Poincar\'e gauge theory with \TIDAL{}, we find both \textbf{ghost-free
  amplification} and suppression of the conversion; a curvature--torsion
  derivative coupling, $\MAGFt{}\times\rcD{}\MAGT{}$, is the most consistent
  amplifier --- mapping out which interactions any future bound on the
  Gertsenshtein signal would target.
\end{alertblock}
