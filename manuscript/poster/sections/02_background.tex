\begin{block}{Background}
      \begin{itemize}
            \item GW astronomy spans twelve decades in frequency but the mega-to-gigahertz band
                  lies above any known source. Any signal there would be primordial or
                  beyond-the-Standard-Model.
            \item Proposed detection schemes rely on the Gertsenshtein effect~[1,2], with
                  astrophysical fields~[3,4] and reinterpretations of dark-matter haloscopes~[5].
                  \textbf{No conversion has been confirmed}.
            \item The mixing length is no shorter than $10^{6}\,$km even at magnetar fields.
      \end{itemize}

      as proposed for the GW190425\,/\,FRB\,20190425A coincidence~[6].}
\end{block}
