\begin{block}{Background}
      \begin{itemize}
            \item Gravitational-wave astronomy spans twelve decades in frequency but the
                  mega-to-gigahertz band lies above any known source, any signal there would be
                  primordial or beyond-the-Standard-Model.
            \item Several proposed detection schemes rest on the Gertsenshtein effect~[1,2].
            \item Searches reinterpret dark-matter haloscopes~[4] and astrophysical fields~[3,5],
                  but \emph{no conversion has been confirmed}.
            \item In Einstein--Maxwell gravity the mixing length is no shorter than $10^{6}\,$km
                  even at magnetar fields.
      \end{itemize}
