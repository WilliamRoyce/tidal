## What this pass does

App H §C currently catalogues PGT × electromagnetism cubic completions
(`pgt_enumeration.tex:269–291`) and Chern–Simons torsion–EM couplings
(`pgt_enumeration.tex:293–318`) entirely in index-contraction prose.
This pass adds **vertex-topology figures** that visualise the structural
distinctions, one short connecting sentence per figure, and a single
caveat paragraph framing diagrams as schematic classifiers (not
Feynman-rule evaluations).

## Decision rule for figure count

A panel earns inclusion when **all three** of these hold:

- (a) **Distinct vertex topology** — the leg structure differs from
  every other panel already in the figure.
- (b) **Distinct physical interpretation** — the linearised role differs
  (3-vertex new conversion channel, 2-point self-energy / mass
  correction, parity-odd channel, etc.).
- (c) **Not adequately conveyed by prose** — the structural distinction
  is *not* visible in the index-contraction text without the reader
  reconstructing the topology.

A panel that fails (a) collapses with the panel it duplicates; that
fails (b) but passes (a) becomes a colour/label variant; that fails (c)
is dropped.

### Figure H.cs — `figures/vertex_topology_cs.tex`

Single panel, three labelled $T$-legs ($T^{(1)}, T^{(2)}, T^{(3)}$) on a
single CS-vertex topology. The three couplings $\chi^{\text{CS}}_{1,2,3}$
are structurally identical (same $\varepsilon\,T\!A\!F$ leg structure,
parity-odd) and differ only by which irreducible torsion sector enters.
Criterion (a) fails for separate panels; treat as one structural object
with three labelled variants. Captions distinguish the physics
($\chi^{\text{CS}}_2$ sources $\bm A\!\cdot\!\bm B$ helicity;
$\chi^{\text{CS}}_3$ uniquely selects spin-2 torsion).

## Leg-labelling convention

- Graviton $h$: coil-decorated line, label $h$.
- Photon $A$: snake-decorated line, label $A$.
- Torsion $T$: dashed line, label $T$.
- Background field: ×-terminated external leg, label $\bar F$.
- Vertex: filled dot with coupling-family letter ($\lambda$, $\kappa$,
  $\rho$, $\mu$, $\chi^{\text{CS}}$).

Dynamical external legs carry the bare field symbol (no "$\delta$"
prefix); the leg-style itself plus the explicit $\bar F$ convention
encodes the dynamical-vs-background distinction. Caption opens with
"all dynamical external legs denote linearised perturbations of the
field they label; $\bar F$ denotes the background magnetic field
$\Bzero$".

## Caveat paragraph

Single paragraph at the start of App H §C ("Cubic completions"):

> The vertex-topology diagrams below are schematic classifiers: each
> panel summarises the leg structure and field content of a linearised
> vertex generated by background insertion on a quadratic or cubic
> operator. The conversion amplitudes used in the survey are computed
> classically from the kinetic matrix $\mathcal{K}(\Bzero)$ of
> \cref{KineticMatrix}, not from Feynman-rule evaluation; the diagrams
> visualise vertex topology and $\Bzero$-counting, in the spirit of the
> EFT-of-gravity literature review~\cite{burgess2003quantum}. The
> classical caveat on identifying $F_{\mu\nu}$ in curved backgrounds is
> treated separately~\cite{hwangnoh2023graviton}.
